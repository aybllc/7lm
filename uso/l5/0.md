# L5 - Interpretation, Review, and Publication

L5 is the human-facing layer: interpretation, review, governance, ratification, and publication
discourse about the corpus. Everything here is talk about the lower layers and never a substitute
for them. Discussion at L5 can decide what to do; it cannot change what a lower layer means or what
its evidence supports.

## Manual work

1. Name the exact lower-layer artifact and version under discussion before discussing it.
2. Say which act this entry performs or records - a record of an adjudication the layer owner
   made, a ratification, or a validation - and do not put two under one heading.
3. For an adjudication *record*, state the scope the owner settled it for and who the layer owner
   is. A settled meaning is scoped, not universal.
4. For a ratification, record who ratified, what state, on what date, and under what authority.
5. For validation, record whether independent external evidence establishes scientific validity as
   /uso/l0/definitions/0.md defines it, naming the evidence and where it lives; if there is none,
   say so plainly.
6. Name the artifact's epistemic status as /uso/l0/epistemic records it; never restate or change
   it here. If new evidence bears on it, record the evidence and send it to the owning layer,
   which changes the status.
7. Send any disagreement about a lower-layer meaning down to that layer's owner; do not settle it by
   wording chosen here.
8. Before publication, check that every claim carries its epistemic status, every definition its
   definition status, and that no summary sentence upgrades either.

## Three acts

| Act | Settles | Does not settle | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adjudication | Nothing by itself: L5 RECORDS an adjudication the layer owner made, naming the owner and the scope | Which meaning controls — that is the owning layer's act, never L5's | Project rule |
| Ratification | That an owner accepts a state as it stands | Whether the state is supported by evidence | Project rule |
| Validation (scientific-validity check) | Whether independent evidence establishes `scientific validity`, as /uso/l0/definitions/0.md defines it, for a claim | Which meaning controls, or who accepts it | Project rule |

An adjudication is made once, by the artifact's layer owner, in the file that owner keeps - for
a definition /uso/l0/definitions, for an interpretation /uso/l0/semantics. What L5 holds is the
record OF that adjudication: the review that led to it, the owner, and the scope it was ratified
for, naming the owner's entry rather than restating its content.

Neither `validation` nor `scientific validity` is coined here. Both are owned at
/uso/l0/definitions/0.md and keep the sense stated there: `validation` in its own right, and
`scientific validity` for support by independent evidence adequate to the claim type. Compare
ISO/IEC/IEEE 24765:2017(E), Clause 3.4500 'validation', definition 1, printed page 499: "1.
confirmation, through the provision of objective evidence, that the requirements for a specific
intended use or application have been fulfilled". That definition is about fulfilling stated
requirements; it says nothing about this scaffold.

## Boundary

- Rhetoric cannot change lower-layer meaning, definition status, or epistemic status.
- Ratification records a decision. It does not upgrade evidence, close a failure, or create validity.
- Review volume, consensus, seniority, and publication venue are not validation.
- Do not create or redefine terms here; definitions are owned at the layer that can state them.
- Do not resolve a contradiction, tension, or inconsistency by rewording it.
- Do not admit new material here; new material enters at L7 ingress.

When a difficulty here is about the process rather than about the artifact under review, record it
in /L7/process-lessons.md.
