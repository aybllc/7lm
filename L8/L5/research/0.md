# L8/L5/research/

**Object:** Directory for the active research record and discourse owned at L5.
**Path:** `L8/L5/research/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** L8/L5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for the active research record and discourse owned at L5. It is where the layer's core question — "What may responsibly be concluded, questioned, taught, compared, or decided from the available work?" — is worked on the record: research questions, hypotheses, rationale, analyses, interpretations, and findings, together with literature search and relevance judgment and the claim scope, limitations, uncertainty, explanation, and conclusion that attach to each claim.

This directory separates three recurring L5 research representations: lines of inquiry under `lanes/`, review under `review/`, and findings under `findings/`. Research activity here compares claims with evidence, interprets, criticizes, qualifies, rejects, accepts, or supersedes claims. When an L5 judgment depends on a changed lower-layer representation, that lower-layer change is represented at the layer that owns it rather than being hidden inside L5 interpretation.

## Why it exists

Keeps inquiry and interpretation separate from institutional participant structure. The research record is about what is claimed and on what evidence; who holds which participant position is filed at `L8/L5/institution/`.

## Holds

- distinct lines of inquiry — `L8/L5/research/lanes/`
- review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims — `L8/L5/research/review/`
- findings that may responsibly be stated from the available work and evidence — `L8/L5/research/findings/`
- literature search and relevance judgment, and the disclosure of methods, exclusions, uncertainty, and inferential limits that each claim carries

## Does not hold

- the work product itself: writing, experiments, computation as performed — `L8/L4/work/`
- exact relations, operators, proofs — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`
- approximation, uncertainty models, tolerances as engineering objects — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`
- admissible states — `L8/L1/state-space/`
- definitions used by the bounded object — `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- institutional participant positions — `L8/L5/institution/`
- retained history, superseded or retired material — `L8/L6/`
- external provenance, governance, peer relationships, and outward acts — `L7/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 may interpret definitions and other lower-layer representations regardless of source. Interpretation does not rewrite the underlying L0 meaning, provenance relation, or any separate peer relationship. The layer's hard boundary applies to everything filed here: consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact. A claim filed here is no broader than its evidence and stated maturity.
