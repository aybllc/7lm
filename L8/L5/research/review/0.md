# L8/L5/research/review/

**Object:** Directory for review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims.
**Path:** `L8/L5/research/review/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** L8/L5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims. Review compares a claim with its evidence and dependencies and may support, reject, qualify, question, or supersede the claim's L5 standing. The claim under review remains in the position that owns the claim; the review is a separate L5 representation naming what it reviews.

A review here identifies the claim reviewed, the dependencies considered, the review outcome, and the review standing. If review exposes a defect in a lower-layer representation, the review records that dependency rather than silently rewriting the lower-layer object. The lower-layer correction remains a representation of the layer where the defect occurs.

## Why it exists

Keeps review activity distinguishable from the findings being reviewed. A reader can then see a finding, the review of it, and the review status the finding carries as three separate things.

## Holds

- review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims
- the review status a claim carries, as the layer's output requires ("cited dependencies, limitations, review status, and reproducibility information")
- the outcome or standing produced by review — support, reject, qualify, question, or supersede
- identified lower-layer dependencies or defects exposed by review
- temporary intake material awaiting semantic classification when this repository chooses to use review as a catch-all (see below)

## Does not hold

- the claims and lines of inquiry under review — `L8/L5/research/lanes/`
- the findings under review — `L8/L5/research/findings/`
- the L4 evidence a review re-checks — `L8/L4/work/`
- proof or measurement; review cannot supply either — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`, `L8/L4/work/`
- peer participant positions, including a reviewer's institutional position — `L8/L5/institution/peers/`
- superseded reviews and retired claims — `L8/L6/`
- external/public legal and access governance — `L7/governance/`

## Temporary intake exemplar

This repository may use this review position as a temporary catch-all for material awaiting classification or owner review. That is **temporary custody**, not semantic ownership. Material waiting here does not become L5 merely because it has not yet been classified; once its role is known, its representation belongs at the layer that states it correctly.

Labels such as `PARKED`, movement between an inbox and a lane, or a particular deletion/retention practice are implementation choices of a repository using this intake pattern. They are not part of the agnostic definition of L5 review.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 review may interpret definitions and other representations regardless of source. Review does not rewrite the underlying semantic definition, provenance relation, or any separately represented peer relationship. Consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact.
