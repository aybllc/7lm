# L8/L5/research/findings/

**Object:** Directory for findings that may responsibly be stated from the available work and evidence.
**Path:** `L8/L5/research/findings/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** L8/L5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for findings that may responsibly be stated from the available work and evidence. A finding is an L5 judgment: it is what the layer concludes after comparing a claim to the L4 work and evidence and to the relevant L3–L0 contracts. It is not the raw L4 output, and it is not the proof, measurement, or lower-layer fact it rests on; it cites them.

In practice a person filing here states the finding, its cited dependencies, its scope and limitations, its uncertainty, and its review status, and discloses the methods, exclusions, and inferential limits behind it — the form the layer's output requires for L6 retention and L7 presentation. A finding is no broader than its evidence and maturity. A finding that review later rejects or supersedes is qualified or superseded here as an L5 act; the finding as it stood is retained at `L8/L6/`.

## Why it exists

Keeps stated findings tied to L5 judgment rather than treating raw L4 output as a conclusion. A computation run, an experiment performed, or a manuscript written at `L8/L4/work/` becomes a finding only when L5 judges what it supports.

## Holds

- findings that may responsibly be stated from the available work and evidence
- for each finding: cited dependencies, limitations, uncertainty, explanation, conclusion, review status, and reproducibility information
- a finding that the available work does not support a claim, stated with the same disclosure

## Does not hold

- the work and evidence a finding rests on — `L8/L4/work/`
- the line of inquiry that produced it — `L8/L5/research/lanes/`
- the review, criticism, or audit of it — `L8/L5/research/review/`
- exact relations or proofs a finding invokes — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`
- tolerances, uncertainty models, and feasibility conditions a finding relies on — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`
- a definition; a finding may fix what a definition's consequences are, but the definition stays at `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/` or, for a peer definition, at its `L7/peering/` binding
- refuted or superseded findings as retained history — `L8/L6/`
- the released form of a finding — `L7/allications/publications/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 may interpret externally owned definitions, but interpretation does not transfer ownership of the peer definition into L0. Consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact. A finding about a lower-layer object that requires different lower-layer meaning is returned to that owner as an explicit revision; it is not reinterpreted in place here.
