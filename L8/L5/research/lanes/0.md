# L8/L5/research/lanes/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for distinct lines of inquiry within the research record. Each lane is one question or investigative path — its research questions, hypotheses, rationale, analyses, and interpretations — kept apart from every other lane so that what one path concludes is not read as what another path concluded. A lane frames a question and interprets the evidence it gathers; it compares claim to evidence and states the claim's scope, limitations, and uncertainty.

A lane represents one line of inquiry and the L4 work/evidence and lower-layer representations on which that inquiry depends. Claims or thesis statements belonging to the inquiry remain associated with the lane that asserts them. Review of those claims is represented at `L8/L5/research/review/`; findings reached from the inquiry are represented at `L8/L5/research/findings/`.

## Why it exists

Prevents separate questions or investigative paths from being collapsed into one undifferentiated research stream. Each lane keeps its own question and its own claims; a claim is read as the claim of the lane that asserts it.

## Holds

- distinct lines of inquiry, one lane per question or investigative path
- research questions, hypotheses, rationale, analyses, interpretations belonging to a lane
- research claims and thesis statements, as claims of the lane that asserts them, with cited dependencies, limitations, and review status
- literature search and relevance judgment performed within a lane

## Does not hold

- review, criticism, audit, or qualification of a lane's claims — `L8/L5/research/review/`
- findings stated from the available work — `L8/L5/research/findings/`
- the work and evidence a lane interprets — `L8/L4/work/`
- proofs and exact relations a claim depends on — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`
- definitions a claim's terms depend on — `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- external antecedents (prior art) — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`
- superseded lanes and retired claims — `L8/L6/`

## The working position

A lane is also where an item goes when the owner takes it up from the catch-all `L8/L5/research/review/`, and it goes back there if set down again. Position is state; the two moves are recorded by the repository's history and nothing is deleted.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 may interpret definitions regardless of source. Interpretation does not rewrite the underlying semantic definition, source provenance, or a separately represented peer relationship. Consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact.
