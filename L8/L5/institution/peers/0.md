## What this directory is

This is the directory for peer participant positions within the institutional setting. A peer position is a participant role in the research conducted at `L8/L5/research/` — a participant in review, audit, research governance, or teaching — distinguished from the research-owner role and from undergraduate positions. The position is filed here; the review, criticism, or claim the peer produces is filed at `L8/L5/research/review/` or the corresponding research directory.

This directory represents a peer participant position and the role it carries in review, audit, research governance, or teaching. The position supplies institutional context for a participant's judgment; it does not add evidential weight. Consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact.

## Why it exists

Keeps peer participation distinct from other institutional roles. A review can then be read with its author's position known and kept separate from the position of the research owner whose claim is under review.

## Holds

- peer participant positions within the institutional setting
- the role a peer position carries in review, audit, research governance, or teaching

## Does not hold

- the review a peer writes — `L8/L5/research/review/`
- claims and findings a peer contributes — `L8/L5/research/lanes/`, `L8/L5/research/findings/`
- undergraduate positions — `L8/L5/institution/undergraduates/`
- an external counterpart relationship as such — `L7/peering/`. The word peer is used here for an institutional participant role and at L7 for an external counterpart relation; the two roles may coincide in an example but are not identical by name alone.
- access relations and permissions — `L7/governance/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

An L5 peer participant may interpret definitions and other research representations, but that participant role does not alter the semantic layer, source provenance, or any separate L7 peer relationship.
