## What this directory is

This is the directory for undergraduate participant positions within the institutional setting. An undergraduate position is a participant role in the research and teaching conducted at L5, distinguished from peer positions and from the research-owner role. The position is filed here; any claim, analysis, or review an undergraduate participant produces is filed at `L8/L5/research/` under the same rules as any other L5 claim.

This directory represents the undergraduate participant position and the role it carries in the research setting. Work produced by an undergraduate participant is represented by the same L5 research categories as work produced by any other participant; the participant label itself adds no evidential weight.

## Why it exists

Keeps undergraduate participation distinct from peer and research-owner roles. The three roles are then separately readable in the record, and a claim is read with its author's position known.

## Holds

- undergraduate participant positions within the institutional setting
- the role an undergraduate position carries in research participation and teaching

- teaching material as produced writing — `L8/L4/work/writing/`; its research meaning stays L5

- access relations and permissions — `L7/governance/`

**Research note.** Forward-facing discourse forums, chat, and institutional documentation may allow fellows or participants to operate under a shared institutional identity while access controls separate participant roles and lower-layer discourse backends.

## Boundary / traversal rule

An L5 undergraduate participant may interpret definitions and other research representations, but that participant role does not alter the semantic layer, source provenance, or any separately represented L7 peer relationship.
