## What this directory is

This is the directory for the institutional research setting and participant positions. It holds the social/institutional setting in which the research at `L8/L5/research/` is conducted: which participant positions exist, and how the roles of peers, undergraduates, and the research owner are distinguished. The layer owns review, audit, research governance, and teaching; this directory holds the participant structure through which those are carried, not the research record itself.

This directory represents participant positions within the institutional research setting — including peer positions under `L8/L5/institution/peers/` and undergraduate positions under `L8/L5/institution/undergraduates/` — separately from the claims, reviews, or findings those participants produce under `L8/L5/research/`. A participant position supplies role context, not evidential weight; consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact.

## Why it exists

Keeps the social/institutional setting distinct from the research record itself. Who participated, and in what role, is answered here; what was concluded, and on what evidence, is answered at `L8/L5/research/`.

## Holds

- the institutional research setting
- participant positions: peer positions (`L8/L5/institution/peers/`) and undergraduate positions (`L8/L5/institution/undergraduates/`)
- the distinction between peer, undergraduate, and research-owner roles

## Does not hold

- the research record: lanes, review, findings — `L8/L5/research/`
- teaching as research meaning — owned at `L8/L5/` (layer sheet, Owns / represents); this directory holds only the participant positions
- external counterpart relationships represented at the surface — `L7/peering/`; an L5 participant role is not automatically an L7 peer relation
- the external/public legal and access relation — `L7/governance/`
- ACL/role constraints and institutional access as machine-readable metadata — directory metadata described at `L8/L5/`; not instantiated in this repository
- a forum or chat interface as realized and operated — `L8/L4/work/`; as exposed — `L7/`
- the record of how participation developed over time — `L8/L6/history/`

## In the case of

**Research note.** Forward-facing discourse forums, chat, and institutional documentation may allow fellows or participants to operate under a shared institutional identity while access controls separate participant roles and lower-layer discourse backends.

**Standing.** A forum or chat interface may be realized operationally at L4 and exposed at L7, while institutional roles, discourse, interpretation, review, and research meaning carried through it remain L5-owned. L7 governance concerns the external/public legal and access relation, not scientific judgment.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 owns institutional and research-discourse meaning. An external participant may also appear in an L7 peering relation when the external relationship itself is represented, but the two roles are not identical. Machine-readable ACL or institutional-access information may govern an implementation without creating the research meaning, and none is instantiated in this repository.

- The word "institution" here means the institutional research setting as the specification's prose states it; the specification does not name any actual institution, and this sheet does not.
