## What this directory is

"Directory for prior states, branch history, corrections, failures, unresolved paths, and reconstructable development over time." A record filed here answers how the object changed: which prior internal state existed, what corrected or superseded it, what failed, and what remains unresolved. It is written so that an inspector arriving from `L7/` can reconstruct that development before entering the active payload.

This directory represents the internal history needed to reconstruct how the bounded object changed: prior states, branches, corrections, supersession, retirement, and other internal transitions. The architectural requirement is reconstructability of the history represented here, not a universal no-deletion mechanism. External source and provenance relations remain at L7 where relevant. Historical retention does not make an L5 research judgment and does not establish truth.

## Why it exists

"Preserves how the object changed instead of presenting only the latest state."

A repository shows its latest state by default; this directory keeps the earlier states and the relations between them addressable.

## Holds

- Records of prior internal states and versions, and of the changes between them.
- Branch history and decision history.
- Failed paths and unresolved obligations, recorded as such.
- Corrections and supersession, with the prior state retained rather than erased.
- Reconstructable development over time: dated chronologies of displacement events.
- In this repository: `L8/L6/history/harmonization-2026-09-04.md`, the record of the harmonization displacement event — inherited terms with prior and current paths, the chronology of deletions and moves already made on `main`, and the list of reserved terms with no current path.
- `specification-history.md` — the specification's versions, the dated lists of what changed in each, the owner's words behind each rule, and the exemplars' snapshot commits: what the specification carried about its own past until 2026-09-07, pulled out so that the specification states the architecture as it is.
- `sheets-2026-09-07.md` — what the sheets carried about their past until 2026-09-07 (the prior scaffold's texts and where they went, deletions by commit, the owner's words quoted beside rules, the exemplar snapshots at pre-7LM commits), pulled out verbatim so that each sheet states its directory as it stands.

## Does not hold

- Retired or superseded internal states and bindings that are no longer current — `L8/L6/retired/` (the spec's retired/ Prose cell).
- External source, authority, prior-art, and public provenance — `L7/provenance/`. A history record may reference a source; the reference resolves to `L7/` and is not re-owned here.
- The research judgment about a prior state — `L8/L5/`.
- Git history. Git records repository change events; a commit identifier in a record here is an orientation pointer, not the scientific memory. The memory is the record.

## Boundary / traversal rule

For an externally encountered object, L7 is the boundary and owns its external provenance/governance relation. L6 is the first interior layer used to reconstruct prior internal states, changes, failures, supersession, and retirement before deeper inspection.
