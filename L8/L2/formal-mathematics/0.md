## What this directory is

Directory for the exact formal system: symbols, types, models, derivations, and obligations. It is the single owner of the bounded thing's exact mathematics inside `L8/L2/`, and its three children — `relations/`, `operators/`, and `proofs/` — separate the relational structure, the lawful actions, and the warrant for exact claims from one another.

What occurs here is exact formal work over the L1 state-space contract at `L8/L1/state-space/`: symbols and types are stated, exact relations and operators are defined, models and carriers are constructed, and derivations or proof obligations are made explicit. Symbols resolve to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility. Functions and operators state their domain and codomain; a partial operation additionally states the subset on which it is defined. General relations state the carrier or product over which the relation is defined rather than being forced into function terminology. Exact claims are supported by derivation or proof, or remain explicitly unproved. A missing L0 meaning or L1 admissibility condition is a lower-layer dependency, not something repaired by L2 prose.

## Why it exists

Keeps formal mathematics grouped under one exact owner before engineering choices are introduced. Engineering choices are introduced at `L8/L3/`; the exact statement they act on is filed here first.

## Holds

- formal symbols and types
- exact models, carriers, and transition systems, stated over the L1 state-space contract
- axioms and definitions in their formal form, bound to L0 meaning
- exact formulas
- the grouping of relations (`relations/`), operators (`operators/`), and proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations (`proofs/`)
- the exact L2 formal contract handed to `L8/L3/`: behavior, invariants, formulas, and unresolved proof obligations

## Does not hold

- semantic and foundational meaning of the symbols — `L8/L0/semantics/` and `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- admissible and excluded states, dimensions, invariants, identity conditions — `L8/L1/state-space/`
- approximation and discretization — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/approximation/`
- numerical uncertainty and error characterization — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/uncertainty/`
- engineering tolerance and admissible engineering bounds — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/tolerances/`
- computation as actually run, and any measurement or realized output — `L8/L4/work/computation/` and `L8/L4/work/`
- research interpretation of what a formal result means — `L8/L5/research/`
- superseded prior formal states and their history — `L8/L6/retired/` and `L8/L6/history/`
- external-source provenance for formal results — `L7/provenance/`; a peering relation at `L7/peering/` only when an identifiable external counterpart relationship is also represented

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership. The layer's hard boundary applies to every file here: no engineering tolerance, approximation quality, physical realization, measurement, or empirical truth is claimed here.
