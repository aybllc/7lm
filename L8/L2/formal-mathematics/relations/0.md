## What this directory is

Directory for exact relations among admitted formal objects. A relation filed here holds between objects that `L8/L1/state-space/` admits and that `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/` has given formal symbols and types; it states what holds exactly, not what acts (that is `operators/`) and not why the claim is warranted (that is `proofs/`).

A relation here is stated over its relevant carrier or Cartesian product, for example (Rsubseteq A	imes B). Totality, functionality, or partiality are stated only when the relation has the corresponding structure; they are not requirements of a general relation. Symbols resolve to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility where those dependencies apply. An asserted relation without proof or derivation remains explicitly unproved and may carry a proof obligation at `proofs/`. Exact relations may include units or dimensioned quantities. Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, numerical uncertainty, and measurement realization remain L3 or L4 questions as appropriate.

## Why it exists

Keeps relational structure distinguishable from the operators that act and the proofs that warrant claims. Separating the three lets an inspector locate the first failing element — the relation, the operation, or the warrant — without reading the others.

## Holds

- exact relations among admitted formal objects, including exact formulas stated as relations
- the carrier, product, type constraints, or other formal context over which each relation is defined
- the binding of each relation's symbols to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility

## Does not hold

- operators, functions, gates, constructors, and other lawful formal actions — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the admissible and excluded states the relations range over — `L8/L1/state-space/`
- the meaning of the related terms — `L8/L0/semantics/`
- approximate, tolerant, numerically realized, or engineering-constrained forms of a relation — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`; exact dimensioned or unit-bearing relations may remain L2
- a relation as computed or measured — `L8/L4/work/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership.
