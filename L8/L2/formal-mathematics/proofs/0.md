# L8/L2/formal-mathematics/proofs/

## What this directory is

Directory for proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations. It holds the warrant for exact claims made in `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`, `relations/`, and `operators/`, and — with equal standing — the explicit record of what has not been warranted. The layer's third acceptance row is the rule of this directory: exact results are supported by proof/derivation or marked as unproved.

This directory represents proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unresolved proof obligations over the L1 state-space contract. A conjecture or unproved obligation remains visible as an L2 formal state rather than being treated as a proved result. If a claim requires a different L1 state space, that dependency belongs to L1; widening the state space inside a proof would change the object being proved.

## Why it exists

Keeps warrant for exact claims inspectable and separate from the claim’s notation or later numerical realization. A reader can then ask of any exact claim whether its warrant exists, where it is, and what it still owes, without confusing the proof with the symbol or with a computed instance.

## Holds

- proofs, lemmas, and derivations of exact claims
- explicit unproved obligations, marked as unproved
- conjectural claims, marked as conjectural
- the unresolved proof obligations that the exact L2 formal contract hands to `L8/L3/`

## Does not hold

- the relations being proved — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- the operators whose properties are proved — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- the admissibility of the states a proof ranges over — `L8/L1/state-space/`
- a numerical check, simulation, or error bound offered in place of a proof — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`
- a proof-checking or test run as executed and its recorded outputs or failures — `L8/L4/work/computation/`
- research judgment about the weight or meaning of a result — `L8/L5/research/review/` and `L8/L5/research/findings/`
- a superseded proof retained for inspection — `L8/L6/retired/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership. No engineering tolerance, approximation quality, physical realization, measurement, or empirical truth is claimed here.
