## What this directory is

Directory for exact operators, functions, gates, constructors, transition maps, and other lawful formal actions. This is where formal action over admitted L1 states is made explicit. Carriers, types, and other formal objects may be defined at the L2 level; this directory holds the operations acting on them. Formal operation begins at L2.

An operator or function here is stated with domain and codomain. If it is partial, the subset on which it is defined is stated in addition to those types. Closure can be represented exactly by asking whether outputs remain within the relevant admitted or formal set. If the operation requires a different L1 state space, that dependency belongs to L1. Numerical realization, approximation error, and engineering tolerance are L3 questions rather than properties supplied by the exact operator merely because it is implemented later.

## Why it exists

Keeps operation explicit rather than burying it inside prose or engineering implementation. An operator that exists only as a sentence in a derivation, or only as a line of code, cannot be inspected on its own domain, codomain, and partiality.

## Holds

- exact operators, functions, gates, constructors, and other lawful formal actions
- transition maps and other exact operations acting on admitted or formally constructed objects
- declared domain and codomain for each operator or function, with the domain of definition stated when the operation is partial
- closure tests, stated exactly against the L1 state-space contract

## Does not hold

- carriers, formal sets, and type structures that are not themselves operations — held at the L2 formal level; exact relations the operators preserve or induce — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- proofs that an operator has its stated properties, and the obligations left open — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the states operated on, and their admissibility — `L8/L1/state-space/`
- algorithms, abstract data-structure choices, discretization, and numerical representation of an operator — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`
- an operator as executed, deployed, or run — `L8/L4/work/computation/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership.
