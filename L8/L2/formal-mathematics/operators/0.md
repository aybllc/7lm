## What this directory is

Directory for exact operators, functions, gates, constructors, and other lawful formal actions. This is where lawful operation over L1 is made explicit: the transition systems, carriers, and actions that `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/` names are given their operators here, each acting on objects that `L8/L1/state-space/` admits. Formal operation begins at L2 (the L1 boundary rule, stated at `L8/L1/`).

In practice, a person filing here declares, for every operator, its domain and codomain or its partiality — the layer's second acceptance row — and binds its symbols to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility. Closure may be tested here: whether the operator's output remains inside the admitted state space. An output that leaves the declared state space is not repaired by the operator; the question returns to `L8/L1/`. Operators are stated exactly; their numerical realization, error, and tolerance are not owned here.

## Why it exists

Keeps operation explicit rather than burying it inside prose or engineering implementation. An operator that exists only as a sentence in a derivation, or only as a line of code, cannot be inspected on its own domain, codomain, and partiality.

## Holds

- exact operators, functions, gates, constructors, and other lawful formal actions
- transition systems and carriers, stated as the operators that act on admitted states
- declared domain/codomain/partiality for each operator
- closure tests, stated exactly against the L1 state-space contract

## Does not hold

- the exact relations the operators preserve or induce — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- proofs that an operator has its stated properties, and the obligations left open — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the states operated on, and their admissibility — `L8/L1/state-space/`
- algorithms, abstract data-structure choices, discretization, and numerical representation of an operator — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`
- an operator as executed, deployed, or run — `L8/L4/work/computation/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership.
