## What this directory is

Directory for the Universality rail content owned by L0. Universality is one of the four rails the L0 sheet names — "Ontological, Semantic, Epistemic, and Universality rail boundaries for this object" — and this directory is where the universality distinctions this bounded object establishes are held, before any admissible state is declared at `L8/L1/`.

In practice a person filing here records a universality distinction this object actually authored, binds it to this rail, states any ambiguity or unresolved extent without filling it by guess, and versions a changed distinction rather than rewriting prior use. A universality claim that depends on a proof, an experiment, a numerical method, or a research conclusion is not stated here; L0 does not own those.

## Why it exists

Preserves Universality as its own rail instead of letting another rail silently supply it. The L0 acceptance check that "No neighboring rail silently supplies another rail's local meaning" is the reason this rail has a directory of its own rather than being folded into ontology or semantics.

## Holds

- Universality-rail distinctions locally authored by this bounded object.
- Statements of ambiguity or unresolved universality, recorded as unresolved.
- Versioned prior universality distinctions where a distinction has changed.
- References to any externally owned universality input, carried as an exact named external semantic binding with object identity, owner, source layer, version, ingress binding, and immutable status.

## Does not hold

- Externally owned definitions copied inward — bound through `L7/peering/` and represented at `L7/provenance/`.
- Ontological, semantic, or epistemic distinctions — `L8/L0/ontology/`, `L8/L0/semantics/`, `L8/L0/epistemic/`.
- Admissible states or possibility bounds — `L8/L1/state-space/`.
- Proofs or exact relations that would establish generality — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`.
- Research conclusions about the object's generality — `L8/L5/research/`.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L0/semantics contains the semantic representation authored by the bounded thing. An externally owned definition is not copied into L0 merely to populate the directory. The same applies to this rail: an external universality statement is not copied here to make the rail look complete.
