## What this directory is

Directory for the Universality rail content owned by L0. Universality is one of the four rails the L0 sheet names — "Ontological, Semantic, Epistemic, and Universality rail boundaries for this object" — and this directory is where the universality distinctions this bounded object establishes are held, before any admissible state is declared at `L8/L1/`.

In practice this rail records universality distinctions that the bounded object actually uses before L1. A distinction may be locally formulated or adopted from an external source. Source origin does not change its L0 rail role; provenance can separately record where it came from. A universality claim that depends on a proof, experiment, numerical method, or research conclusion is not established here; those questions belong above.

## Why it exists

Preserves Universality as its own rail instead of letting another rail silently supply it. Its separate directory makes the universality role explicit rather than folding it silently into ontology or semantics.
