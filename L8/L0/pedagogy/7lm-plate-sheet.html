The eight positions

L8 envelopes them all. L0 is the origin. L1 is the floor and L6 the end of the payload. L7 is the glue. L8’s trailing edge is the connector to the next object’s L0.

Reading the drawing

Pine marks the two faces of the packet: the glue at L7, and the connector at L8’s trailing edge.

Monospace is a real path in the repository. Every position named here carries its own 0.md sheet.

What the drawing does not decide

Whether L7/ moves inside L8/ on disk, or the envelope is semantic and the paths stay as they are. That is the owner’s call, and it is what stands between this drawing and the specification.

Standing rules

  • Nothing is deleted before it is enumerated at L8/L6/history/.
  • Retired sets stay verbatim, headings and all.
  • Higher layers never silently repair lower ones.
