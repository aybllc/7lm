Seven-Layer Model · drawn, not described

The 7LM Plate Sheet

Fifteen plates. Every rule of the model that can be drawn is drawn here, and drawn here only — one sheet to change when the model changes. Plates 01–12 state what the specification ratifies. Plates 13–15 carry a proposal it does not yet contain.

I. The object
01

The eight positions

L7 and L8 are siblings on disk. The seven layers sit flat inside L8; each owns one kind of claim.

02

The count rule

First is always 0. Ordinals are semantics; numbers are magnitudes; the relation between them is is.

03

The names are drawings

L7 draws the desk from its two characters. L8 stacks the interior’s zero over the layers’ zeros.

II. Movement
04

The two directions

Construction climbs from L0 and stops where a dependency is not established. Inspection descends from L7.

05

The bridge, and honest placement

Build whatever the mathematics permits. Claim only what the layer has earned; stopping is placement, not rejection.

06

Containment and expansion

A contained system stays whole at L4. When the question turns to its origin, each answer goes to the layer that owns it.

III. Placement
07

One object, four owners

A single document is filed four times over. No layer holds more than its own aspect of it.

08

The desk, in four branches

Provenance, governance, peering and publications each answer a different question about the outside world.

09

Exposure by direction

The two distinctions are independent. A peer may occupy more than one cell.

10

The route a borrowed definition travels

Source L0, source L7 egress, receiving L7 ingress, receiving L0. Authorship never crosses the boundary.

IV. Operation
11

The catch-all and the lanes

Two moves and two exits. Position is state: the moves are the record, and nothing is deleted along the way.

12

Retire, or enumerate and delete

The rule enforced is integrity, not the absence of deletion. Deletion after enumeration is the ordinary act.

V. Open — not in the specification
13

The packetPROPOSED

One bounded object read as a frame: origin, payload, glue, connector.

14

The chainPROPOSED

Objects joined end to end: the connector of one meets the origin of the next, and the run of them is lineage.

15

What has to be ruledOPEN CALL

The two readings side by side, what neither of them disturbs, and the two questions that decide between them.

Reading the drawings

Pine marks the element carrying the claim in each plate.

Oxide marks what is proposed and not yet ruled — plates 13 to 15 only.

Monospace is a real path in the repository. Every file and folder named here exists.

Open calls on this sheet

  • Does the tree move, or is the enclosure semantic only?
  • What is L8’s job: the check bit, or the envelope whose trailing edge connects to the next L0?

Both are the owner’s to rule. Until then the specification stands as written.

Standing rules

  • Nothing is deleted before it is enumerated at L8/L6/history/.
  • Retired sets stay verbatim, headings and all.
  • Higher layers never silently repair lower ones.
