# L8/L0/semantics/definitions/

**Object:** Directory for definitions that function in the bounded object's Semantic rail.
**Path:** `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/` · **Layer:** L0 — LAYER 0 · **Owner:** L8/L0/

## What this directory is

This directory holds definitions that the bounded object actually uses as definitions within its Semantic rail.

A definition may be locally authored, adopted from an external source, adapted from one or more sources, or retained as an explicitly provisional working meaning. Its semantic role here does not erase or transfer its source authorship. Where source origin matters, provenance represents that external relation.

Peering is not the universal route for external definitions. A definition may arrive through a peer relationship, but it may also be found in a book, article, database, archive, standards source, web search, classroom source, or other external material without any peering relation being represented.

\[
\text{definition role at L0}\neq\text{source provenance}
\]

\[
\text{external source}\not\Rightarrow\text{peer}
\]

## Why it exists

L0 requires a place where the bounded object's operative definitions can be stated and inspected. Keeping definition role at L0 prevents source provenance, authorship, or acquisition method from deciding where the meaning itself belongs.

## Holds

- definitions that function in this bounded object's Semantic rail;
- locally authored definitions;
- explicitly adopted or adapted external definitions used for a stated scope;
- provisional working definitions where the meaning remains unsettled;
- versioned or otherwise distinguishable semantic states when a definition changes;
- the inherited controlled-definitions tables below as the current recorded working meanings of this scaffold.

## Does not hold

- source provenance as provenance — `L7/provenance/`;
- peer relationships merely because a source is external — `L7/peering/` applies only when such a relationship is represented;
- prior-art relation as prior art — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`;
- exact formulas — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`;
- applied or engineering formulas — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`;
- research claims and thesis statements — `L8/L5/research/`.

## Boundary / traversal rule

This directory answers **what definition is functioning here**.

It does not, by that fact alone, answer who originally authored the wording, where the definition was obtained, whether it is authoritative outside its stated scope, or whether a direct peer relationship exists. Those are separate questions.

## Locally authored definitions

- `FIRST_IS_ALWAYS_0.md` — the owner's definition: "First is always 0", with its table (FIRST is 0, SECOND is 1, THIRD is 2, FOURTH is 3). Ordinals are semantics, numbers are magnitudes, and the relation is "is". In this tree it is the rule that makes the model's name and its paths agree: the seven layers are the seven semantics FIRST through SEVENTH and the seven magnitudes `L0` through `L6`, so the seventh layer is `L6`; the first thing at any position is its zero, `0.md`; and 7 is a magnitude, the position of the desk, the eighth reached in traversal, which is why `L7/` is numbered and not a layer.
