## What this directory is

Directory for the Semantic rail content used by this bounded object. The Semantic rail states the meanings under which the object proceeds before state-space distinctions are declared. A semantic representation may be locally formulated, explicitly adopted from an external source, or constructed from both.

In practice, this directory records what the bounded object means by the terms it actually uses. Definitions functioning in that semantic contract go one level down at `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`. Source provenance remains separately representable at L7; peering is used only when a direct external counterpart relationship itself is being represented. Semantic role and textual authorship are therefore different questions.

## Why it exists

Keeps semantic meaning separate from both source provenance and later formal representation. L0 states the meaning used here; L7 provenance can state where that meaning came from; L2 can formalize it exactly.

## Holds

- Semantic-rail representation used by this bounded object.
- The child directory `definitions/` for definitions functioning in that semantic representation.
- Statements of ambiguity or unresolved meaning, recorded as unresolved.
- Changed semantic distinctions represented as changed semantic states when the meaning changes.
- Source references where useful for orientation, without making provenance the semantic definition itself.

## Does not hold

- External-source provenance as provenance — represented at `L7/provenance/`. A source may also participate in peering when an identifiable counterpart relationship is actually represented.
- Prior art — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`.
- Ontological, epistemic, or universality distinctions — `L8/L0/ontology/`, `L8/L0/epistemic/`, `L8/L0/universality/`.
- Admissible states — `L8/L1/state-space/`.
- Exact formulas, relations, operators, or proofs — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/`.
- Applied, tolerant, adaptive, or engineering formulas — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`.
- Research claims and thesis statements — `L8/L5/research/lanes/`.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L0/semantics contains the semantic representation used by the bounded thing. Source origin does not determine semantic layer ownership. A definition may be locally formulated or externally adopted; provenance and peering remain separate external-relation questions.
