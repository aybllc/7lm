## What this directory is

Directory for admissible and excluded states, dimensions, invariants, identity conditions, and static compatibility. It is the one child of `L8/L1/` and the place where the L1 state-space contract is written: the output the layer sheet defines as "An L1 state-space contract defining what L2 may represent and operate upon."

In practice, a person filing here does one of the layer's permitted actions — "admit, exclude, distinguish, bound, freeze", "test state membership and compatibility", or "issue a new version when the possibility landscape changes" — and nothing else. Every admitted state is grounded in L0 (`L8/L0/`). Invariants are mutually compatible or the collision is explicit. Changes return to L1 and create a new version.

## Why it exists

Establishes what may exist or be represented before lawful operation is introduced. The layer's rationale follows from this: keeping static possibility here "Keeps static possibility separate from the formal operations that begin at L2."

## Holds

- admissible and excluded states
- dimensions, domains, ranges, equivalence classes, identity conditions
- static invariants and compatibility constraints
- the state-space version issued when the possibility landscape changes ("Changes return to L1 and create a new version."); an out-of-space result "motivates an explicit proposal for a revised state space" and the revision, when adopted, is that new version
- an explicit record of any invariant collision ("Invariants are mutually compatible or the collision is explicit.")

## Does not hold

- the semantic/foundational contract the states are grounded in — `L8/L0/` (definitions used by the bounded object at `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`)
- carrying, transition, selection, learning, calculation, propagation, or transformation — "Formal operation begins at L2": `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/` (relations/, operators/, proofs/)
- approximation, numerical representation, tolerance, uncertainty, and feasibility — `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/`
- realized work, computation as run, or written artifacts — `L8/L4/work/`
- research judgment about whether an admitted state is scientifically warranted — `L8/L5/research/`
- superseded state-space versions kept as internal history — `L8/L6/`
- external-source provenance for state-space representations — `L7/provenance/`. If an externally sourced state-space representation actually functions as the bounded object's L1 state space, its L1 role is represented here; peering applies only when a counterpart relationship is also represented.
- the reserved terms "origidity; certainty; intolerance" — reserved as candidate L1 semantics with topology unadjudicated; this directory is not their home

## Boundary / traversal rule

L1 establishes the admissible state space. Formal operation begins at L2.

Hard boundary of the layer: No carrying, transition, selection, learning, calculation, propagation, or transformation. Formal operation begins at L2. Directional inputs: Construction: the current L0 contract. Inspection: higher-layer evidence may trigger a question or proposed revision, but cannot silently change admitted states.
