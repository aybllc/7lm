# Admissibility of the additional probe inputs

The numerical N/U probe admits pairs(n,u) in Q x Q_nonnegative, a declared rational subset of the source's real carrier. Negative radii are rejected by this consumer's admission gate, not silently coerced or treated as permission to alter the original constructor. The product comparison admits two independent input intervals. Shared-variable dependence would be a different target.

The Semantic Magnitude probe admits named semantic labels with nonnegative rational magnitude. Labels `negative` and `zero` are distinct elements of this synthetic instance of Sigma; both may have magnitude zero. This does not equate semantic orientation with numerical sign. Missing semantic operations are unresolved objects, not numerical zero.

These declarations contain no transitions or propagation method. The exact relations and open obligations are at L2; numerical realization choices are at L3.
