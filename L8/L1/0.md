# L8/L1/

**Object:** Admissible states and static possibility bounds.
**Path:** `L8/L1/` · **Layer:** L1 — STATE SPACE · **Owner:** L8/L1/ — this layer owns the admissible state-space representation grounded in L0: what may exist or be represented before lawful operation is introduced.

L1 is the layer that freezes the board. Under the L0 contract established at `L8/L0/`, L1 states which states and distinctions are possible or admissible, which are excluded, and which invariants and identity conditions hold statically. It performs no operation on those states. The spec's analytic view states the separation directly: "L0 is deliberately separated from L1. L0 establishes the local semantic/foundational contract; L1 establishes admissible states under that contract." The same analytic views place L1 in the "L3 / L2 / L1 technical-possibility bundle — Engineering mathematics, exact formalization, and admissible state space can be examined together when the question requires all three dependencies," and note that "L3-L2 is a tighter engineering/formal sub-bundle when L1 is already pinned and not itself under question"; "These bundles are semantic and dependency views, not filesystem nesting and not mandatory traversal paths." The core question is: "What states and distinctions are possible or admissible under the established L0 contract?"

### Owns / represents
- admissible and excluded states
- dimensions, domains, ranges, equivalence classes, identity conditions
- static invariants and compatibility constraints

### Permitted actions
- admit, exclude, distinguish, bound, freeze
- test state membership and compatibility
- issue a new version when the possibility landscape changes

### Acceptance / boundary check
- Every admitted state is grounded in L0.
- Invariants are mutually compatible or the collision is explicit.
- Changes return to L1 and create a new version.

## In the case of

In the case of: State-space containment and scientific claim boundaries

(The spec prints the four headings below at H2; their depth is reduced here only to nest under this sheet's section. Words are unchanged.)

### State-space boundary — leaving the declared state space

A valid object or transition must remain inside the declared state space and its admissibility rules.

If a result falls outside the declared state space, then either:

- the construction is invalid under the current model; or
- the current state space is incomplete for the phenomenon, and a new explicitly justified extension must be proposed.

The model may not silently step outside its state space and still claim continuity with the same system.

### Mathematical permission is not automatic scientific or engineering permission

A mathematical construction is allowed to remain mathematically valid even when no engineering realization exists.

The construction stops at the first unresolved required bridge.

That is not rejection. It is honest placement.

Example: peer-reviewed FTL/warp-drive mathematics may be legitimate mathematics while still lacking an engineering bridge. The math can stay where it is. It does not receive a free promotion into realizable propulsion.

### No exotic capability gets a free explanatory advantage

FTL, unlimited power, perfect information, zero latency, infinite endurance, and similar assumptions may be studied conditionally.

They may not be used as free empirical resources unless the capability itself carries its own evidentiary and engineering burden.

The rule became:

> Build whatever the math permits. Claim only what the layer has earned.

### Observable science is bounded without forbidding speculation

The architecture is not anti-speculation.

Hypotheses may be created and mathematically explored.

The discipline is that unobserved or unengineered capability cannot be silently promoted downward into the foundational or physical claim space.

**State-space and claim boundaries**

An object or formal operation may not silently leave the declared state space and still claim continuity with the same L1 contract. An out-of-space result either fails under the current model or motivates an explicit proposal for a revised state space.

Mathematical permission is not automatic engineering or empirical permission. A mathematically valid object may stop at L2. Engineering feasibility must be earned at L3, realized work at L4, and scientific interpretation at L5. Packaging or publicity at L7 never promotes maturity by itself.

The architecture therefore permits speculation while making maturity explicit: build whatever the mathematics legitimately permits; claim only what the completed handoffs have earned.

**Note.** STATE and IMMUTABLE remain useful descriptive/mnemonic language, but the controlling layer name is State Space.

## Direction

Construction enters from `L8/L0/` and leaves toward `L8/L2/`; inspection enters from `L8/L2/` and proceeds toward `L8/L0/`. This follows the spec's two valid directions — scientific construction outward L0→L1→L2→L3→L4→L5→L6→L7, and authorized external inspection inward L7→L6→L5→L4→L3→L2→L1→L0 — and its rule that "Higher layers do not silently repair lower layers": higher-layer evidence may trigger a question or proposed revision of the admitted states, but the change returns to L1 and creates a new version.
