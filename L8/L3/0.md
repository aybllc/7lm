# L8/L3/

**Object:** Approximation, numerical representation, tolerance, uncertainty, and feasibility.
**Path:** `L8/L3/` · **Layer:** L3 — ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS · **Owner:** L8/L3/, the owner of engineering approximation and feasibility applied to the L2 formal contract.

Layer 3 is the "Layer 3 container for numerical and engineering feasibility applied to the L2 formal contract." It receives the exact contract from `L8/L2/` together with declared constraints, and returns to `L8/L4/` a specification of what can be built or run and within what conditions. It does not own the realized output of `L8/L4/`, and it does not create empirical evidence. Its core question is: "How can the exact L2 relation be made feasible under declared constraints?"

**Boundary / traversal rule.** L3 owns engineering approximation and feasibility; it does not own the realized L4 output.

The neighbouring boundary is exactness versus engineering realization. L2 may state exact unit-bearing, dynamical, feedback, control, or adaptive relations. L3 begins where implementation introduces representation choices, approximation, finite precision, tolerances, uncertainty, stability or convergence under implementation, resource constraints, or other feasibility conditions. L4 then owns the realized run, measurement, build, or other work event.

## In the case of

### Working life boundary hypothesis

**Research hypothesis, not layer boundary.** The working statement that "L2 does not mathematically support life" is retained as an untested hypothesis. It does not define the L2/L3 boundary.

Exact relations involving continuation, feedback, control, adaptation, or environmental exchange may be represented at L2 when they are stated exactly. Their engineering realization belongs to L3 when the representation depends on finite precision, approximation, tolerance, uncertainty, numerical stability, implementation constraints, resource bounds, or other feasibility conditions. L4 realizes or records the resulting work.

**Author note.** INFINITE is retained as a working term for open-ended tolerance/refinement across implementations and time, not as permission to move exact L2 objects here.

This hypothesis is carried here exactly as the specification marks it. It is not resolved by this sheet, and nothing filed under `L8/L3/` tests it by being filed.
