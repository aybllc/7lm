# L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/approximation/

**Object:** Directory for approximations and discretizations used to make exact relations workable.
**Path:** `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/approximation/` · **Layer:** L3 — ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS · **Owner:** L8/L3/

## What this directory is

This directory represents approximations and discretizations used to make an exact L2 relation workable under engineering constraints. The approximation, numerical representation, precision, operating assumptions, valid region, and relation to relevant L2 invariants are stated here when those properties define the approximation.

The approximation remains distinct from the exact L2 relation. Its error or uncertainty may be characterized in `../uncertainty/`, tolerances in `../tolerances/`, and resource or cost claims at the L3 level. Use of an approximation does not make it exact.

## Why it exists

"Makes loss or approximation visible instead of letting it appear as exact mathematics." When an exact relation is discretized or replaced by a workable method, the loss is written here where it can be inspected, rather than absorbed into a result at `L8/L4/`.

## Holds

- approximations and discretizations of exact L2 relations
- discretizations and the chosen numerical representation and precision
- the operating assumptions and valid region of each approximation
- comparisons of candidate approximation methods to L2 invariants

## Does not hold

- the exact relation itself — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- the exact operator or constructor being approximated — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- proof that the exact relation holds — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the uncertainty or error characterization of the approximation — `../uncertainty/`
- the declared tolerance the approximation must meet — `../tolerances/`
- the computation, experiment, or writing that realizes the approximation — `L8/L4/work/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L3 owns engineering approximation and feasibility; it does not own the realized L4 output. "Inspection: L4 evidence may force re-evaluation without silently changing L2" — a failed approximation is re-evaluated here; the exact relation at `L8/L2/` is revised only explicitly.
