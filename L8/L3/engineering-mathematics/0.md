# L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/

**Object:** Directory for algorithms, numerical representation, units, precision, feasibility, stability, and complexity.
**Path:** `L8/L3/engineering-mathematics/` · **Layer:** L3 — ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS · **Owner:** L8/L3/

## What this directory is

"Directory for algorithms, numerical representation, units, precision, feasibility, stability, and complexity." It is the single engineering owner under `L8/L3/`: the place where the exact L2 formal contract is translated into implementable numerical/engineering methods, with the three named aspects of that translation held in its children `approximation/`, `uncertainty/`, and `tolerances/`.

A person filing here is doing one of the layer's permitted actions — "approximate, discretize, optimize, estimate, bound error, choose representation", "compare candidate methods to L2 invariants", "state operating assumptions and valid region" — and the record must pass the layer's acceptance check: "Error/uncertainty, units, precision, and tolerance are explicit. Required L2 invariants are preserved within declared bounds. Resource/complexity claims are analyzed or labeled estimates." The children `approximation/`, `uncertainty/`, and `tolerances/` carry the three Prose cells the specification gives them; the remaining items of the layer's Owns row are held at this level.

## Why it exists

"Keeps the engineering translation of exact formal behavior explicit and inspectable." The exact relation stays at `L8/L2/`; what is done to it to make it feasible is written down here rather than left implicit in realized L4 work.

## Holds

- algorithms and abstract data-structure choices
- units, numerical representation, precision, discretization
- error/uncertainty budgets, sensitivity, stability, convergence, complexity
- simulation, optimization, feasibility arguments
- the stated operating assumptions and valid region of each method
- comparisons of candidate methods to L2 invariants
- its three children: `approximation/`, `uncertainty/`, `tolerances/`

## Does not hold

- exact relations — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- exact operators, functions, gates, constructors — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- proofs, derivations, and unproved obligations — `L8/L2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the semantic reading of a formula — `L8/L0/` ("Semantic reading remains L0")
- the realized L4 output — `L8/L4/` (L3 "is not the realized L4 output and does not itself create empirical evidence")
- externally owned standards or source texts relied on by a method — bound through `L7/`, not copied here

## Boundary / traversal rule

L3 owns engineering approximation and feasibility; it does not own the realized L4 output. Construction into this directory comes from "the L2 formal contract plus declared environmental, resource, and performance constraints"; on inspection, "L4 evidence may force re-evaluation without silently changing L2."
