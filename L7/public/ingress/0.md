# ingress — in

Holds: everything arriving — drop-offs, delivered sources, anything handed in.
Being here means: it arrived. Nothing more. Arrival is not provenance and
nothing here is citable.

## Do
- Keep the object unchanged and record where it came from.
- Route it out; ingress holds nothing at rest.
- If it needs the owner, write it into `common/durable/orphan/`.
