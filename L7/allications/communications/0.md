# L7/allications/communications/

**Object:** Directory for communications the bounded object issues outward.
**Path:** `L7/allications/communications/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

A communication is an outward act whose defining feature is conveying information outside the bounded object: a statement, correspondence, announcement, answer, or comparable message. The representation may distinguish content, audience or recipient, time, and version where those properties matter.

## Why it exists

Communication is separately addressable because conveying information is not necessarily publication, application, specification, notification, or participation. One real event may have more than one of these properties; each position states only its own relation.

## Holds

- Outward communications, with content, audience or recipient, timing, and version where those distinctions matter.

## Does not hold

- an issued notice that discharges an obligation — `L7/allications/notifications/`
- a request made of an external body — `L7/allications/applications/`
- a released object with a release identity — `L7/allications/publications/`
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the relationship to a named external counterpart as such — `L7/peering/` when separately represented

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
