# L7/allications/communications/

**Object:** Directory for communications the bounded object issues outward.
**Path:** `L7/allications/communications/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

A communication is an outward act whose substance is the conveying: a statement issued, correspondence sent, an announcement made, an answer given to an external party. What is filed is what was said, to whom, and when.

## Why it exists

Saying something to an outside party is not releasing a publication, not asking for anything, and not discharging an obligation to notify. Each of those has its own position here. A communication is the residual outward act of speech, and it needs a place so that it is not filed as a release it is not.

## Holds

- Communications issued outward, with recipient, date, and the exact content or its version.

## Does not hold

- an issued notice that discharges an obligation — `L7/allications/notifications/`
- a request made of an external body — `L7/allications/applications/`
- a released object with a release identity — `L7/allications/publications/`
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the binding to a named peer — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
