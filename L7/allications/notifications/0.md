# L7/allications/notifications/

**Object:** Directory for notices the object issues outward.
**Path:** `L7/allications/notifications/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for notices the object issues outward." A notification is an outward act in which giving notice is itself the thing required: a disclosure, a required report, a breach or defect notice, an alert to a party who must be told. What is filed is the notice, the party notified, the date, and the obligation it discharges where there is one.

## Why it exists

A notice is not a communication with a formal tone. It usually discharges an obligation, and the record of having given it on a date is the point. Keeping notices separate means the question "was notice given, and when" has one place to be answered.

## Holds

- Notices issued outward, with recipient, date, and the obligation or condition under which the notice was given.

## Does not hold

- the obligation or condition itself, which is a governance fact — `L7/governance/`
- an ordinary statement to an outside party — `L7/allications/communications/`
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the binding to a named peer — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
