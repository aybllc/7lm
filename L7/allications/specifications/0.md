# L7/allications/specifications/

**Object:** Directory for specifications the bounded object issues outward for others to build or check against.
**Path:** `L7/allications/specifications/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

A specification here is a specification as externally issued or exposed by the bounded object: for example, an interface contract, schema, wire format, requirement set, or other prescriptive technical object made available for external use or comparison. The L7 representation concerns the act or state of issuance, not the internal writing process that produced it.

## Why it exists

The specification as produced work may have an L4 representation and its research or argumentative meaning may have an L5 representation. When it is issued outward, that outward issuance is an L7 fact. A peer relation may additionally exist when a specific external counterpart is part of the represented event.

## Holds

- Specifications issued outward, with version, audience, destination, or issue context where those distinctions matter.

## Does not hold

- the specification as a written artifact under development — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- an external specification merely because it is used internally; its semantic/formal role belongs to the interior, its source relation to provenance, and peering only applies when an external counterpart relationship is represented
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the relationship to a named external counterpart as such — `L7/peering/` when separately represented

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
