# L7/allications/specifications/

**Object:** Directory for specifications the bounded object issues outward for others to build or check against.
**Path:** `L7/allications/specifications/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

A specification here is an outward act: a contract handed to an external party so that they can implement, interoperate with, or verify the object. An interface contract, a schema, a wire format, a published requirement set. What is filed is the specification as issued, at the version it was issued at.

## Why it exists

A specification the object writes for itself is produced work at L4 and its meaning is L5's. A specification handed outward becomes a commitment to whoever builds on it, and the version they built against matters afterwards. That outward commitment is an L7 fact.

## Holds

- Specifications issued outward, each at an exact version, with who it was issued to or published for.

## Does not hold

- the specification as a written artifact under development — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- an external specification the object consumes, which is bound reference-only — `L7/peering/*/ingress/peer-1/object-1/` (NO EDIT, NO DELETE)
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the binding to a named peer — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
