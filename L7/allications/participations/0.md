# L7/allications/participations/

**Object:** Directory for the bounded object's participations in external settings.
**Path:** `L7/allications/participations/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

A participation is an outward act of taking part: a conference, a working group, a review panel, a standards body, a collaboration the object joined. What is filed is the participation — which body, in what role, over what period, and what the object contributed or received standing from.

## Why it exists

Taking part somewhere is an external relation with a date and a role, and it is not a publication, an application, or a peer binding. Filing it here keeps "where this object has stood" answerable without it being mistaken for a released work or for a peering relationship with a named object.

## Holds

- Participations in external settings, with the body, the role, the period, and what was contributed.

## Does not hold

- participant positions inside the research setting, which are an interior matter — `L8/L5/institution/peers/`
- a bound peer object exchanged with a body — `L7/peering/`
- anything the participation produced that was then released — `L7/allications/publications/`
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the binding to a named peer — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
