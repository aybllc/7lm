# L7/allications/participations/

**Object:** Directory for the bounded object's participations in external settings.
**Path:** `L7/allications/participations/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

A participation is an externally situated engagement in which the bounded object takes part in a setting outside itself: for example, a conference, working group, review panel, standards activity, collaboration, or other external forum. Participation may be a single event or extend over an interval. The representation may distinguish the setting, role, period, and contribution where those properties matter.

## Why it exists

Participation is separately addressable because taking part in an external setting is not identical to publishing, applying, or maintaining a particular peer exchange. A participation may involve peers and communications while remaining a distinct outward relation.

## Holds

- Participations in external settings, with setting, role, period, and contribution where those distinctions matter.

## Does not hold

- participant positions inside the research setting, which are an interior matter — `L8/L5/institution/peers/`
- a peer relationship with an external counterpart as such — `L7/peering/`
- anything the participation produced that was then released — `L7/allications/publications/`
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- a separately represented peer relation — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
