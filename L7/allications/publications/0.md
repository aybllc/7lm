# L7/allications/publications/

**Object:** Directory for released publication objects at the forward face.
**Path:** `L7/allications/publications/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for released publication objects at the forward face." A publication object here is a released thing with a release identity: the version that left, when, in what form, and under what governance. The layer "owns / represents" "allications: the outward acts, of which publication is one", and of the permitted actions "receive, bind, attribute, disclose, present, publish, release, route", the ones that apply here are present, publish, and release. The directional input is "Outward release: approved L6-backed packages."

A person filing here records the release, not the writing and not the argument. The specification separates the four senses: "Released publications are represented by L7/allications/publications/, while provenance and public/legal governance remain separately addressable"; and the public egress row states that "the released publication object itself is held by L7/allications/publications/." The acceptance check that governs every release is that "Publication or legal status does not strengthen scientific evidence."

## Why it exists

"Keeps publication release at L7 while writing remains L4, research meaning remains L5, and internal history remains L6." One document may exist in all four senses; each sense is owned by the layer that can state it.

## Holds

- Released publication objects with explicit release identity: source, version, authority, permissions, status, and direction where applicable.
- Released corpus/publication identity — the part of the inherited term "corpus" that the Harmonization Record places here.

## Does not hold

- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired releases — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of a released object — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or restriction conditions on a release — `L7/governance/`
- outward exchange toward a specific peer — `L7/peering/public/egress/peer-1/object-1/` or the private egress position
- drafts stamped internal, empty release slots, and material that has not left: none of these is a released publication object

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty. Presence in the agnostic topology establishes admissible capability, not mandatory population; unused now does not mean removed. No released publication object is filed here at present. The harmonized specification is filed as a produced writing artifact at `L8/L4/work/writing/` (see `L8/L4/work/writing/0.md`); its release state, if it is released, would be represented here.
- The public release of this repository is a released publication object in the sense of this position: the tree, the sheets, and the source documents, as one commit that carries no history and no author, made by `.github/release.sh` on a person's dispatch (`.github/workflows/release.yml`) and replacing the release before it. Its release identity is the date in its commit message; its form is a repository holding only the current state. What the public may read is that state and never a diff, a commit, or an author of this repository. Nothing under `L8/L6/history/`, `L8/L6/in/`, or `L8/L6/retired/` leaves with it beyond those positions' own sheets (record: `L8/L6/history/harmonization-2026-09-04.md` §38).
- Inherited term "corpus" — disposition Split and reserved: "Released corpus/publication identity belongs at L7/allications/publications/; the broader inherited term remains reserved where its exact role is not yet adjudicated." Only the released-identity sense is placed here.
- Machine metadata: described at `L7/`; not instantiated in this repository. No automation. The specification's machine-metadata row names "release state" among what metadata may identify; none is enabled here.
