# L7/allications/publications/

**Object:** Directory for released publication objects at the forward face.
**Path:** `L7/allications/publications/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This position represents a publication as an outward release of a distinguishable object. Relevant properties may include which version was released, when, in what form, and through what venue or release context.

Publication here is the release relation, not the act of writing and not the research argument. Provenance and governance remain separately representable where relevant. Publication status does not strengthen the underlying scientific evidence.

## Why it exists

"Keeps publication release at L7 while writing remains L4, research meaning remains L5, and internal history remains L6." One document may exist in all four senses; each sense is owned by the layer that can state it.

## Holds

- Released publication objects with enough release identity to distinguish the represented release; related provenance or governance is represented separately when relevant.
- Released corpus/publication identity — the part of the inherited term "corpus" that the Harmonization Record places here.

## Does not hold

- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired releases — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of a released object — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or restriction conditions on a release — `L7/governance/`
- outward relationship with a specific external counterpart as such — `L7/peering/` when that relationship is also being represented
- drafts stamped internal, empty release slots, and material that has not left: none of these is a released publication object

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty. Presence in the agnostic topology establishes admissible capability, not mandatory population; unused now does not mean removed. No released publication object is filed here at present. The harmonized specification is filed as a produced writing artifact at `L8/L4/work/writing/` (see `L8/L4/work/writing/0.md`); its release state, if the owner releases it, would be represented here.
- Inherited term "corpus" — disposition Split and reserved: "Released corpus/publication identity belongs at L7/allications/publications/; the broader inherited term remains reserved where its exact role is not yet adjudicated." Only the released-identity sense is placed here.
- Machine metadata: described at `L7/`; not instantiated in this repository. No automation. The specification's machine-metadata row names "release state" among what metadata may identify; none is enabled here.
