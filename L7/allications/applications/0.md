# L7/allications/applications/

**Object:** Directory for applications the bounded object makes outward.
**Path:** `L7/allications/applications/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

An application is an outward act in which the object asks something of an external body and the asking itself is the record: an application for funding, for review, for admission to a programme or standards body, or of the object's method to a case outside it. What is filed is the application as made — to whom, when, in what version, under what conditions.

## Why it exists

An application is neither a publication nor a communication. It is directed at a specific external authority, it carries an outcome, and it binds the object to what it claimed while applying. Keeping it distinct stops an application being read as a release and stops its outcome being read as a research finding.

## Holds

- Applications made outward, with the body applied to, the date, the version of what was submitted, and the outcome where one is known.

## Does not hold

- the judgement about whether the application's claim is sound — `L8/L5/research/`
- a correspondence that asks for nothing — `L7/allications/communications/`
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the binding to a named peer — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
