# L7/allications/

**Object:** Directory for the bounded object's outward acts: publications, applications, communications, specifications, notifications, and participations.
**Path:** `L7/allications/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This position was named on 2026-09-19 (record: `L8/L6/history/harmonization-2026-09-04.md` §25). It is one of the four canonical direct L7 branches.

What the six share is not a spelling. Each is a nominalized outward act of the bounded object: publishing, applying, communicating, specifying, notifying, taking part. Each is something the object does toward the outside, once, on a date, leaving a record that can be addressed afterwards. The name, all-ications, pronounced ally-cations, is a mnemonic for that family, not the test for membership.

## Why it exists

A released publication was the only outward act the topology had a position for. A grant application, a conference participation, a notice issued under an obligation, an interface specification handed to someone who must build against it — each is an outward act of the same kind, and each had either no position or the wrong one. This branch gives the family one place and keeps the six senses distinct within it.

## Holds

- The six named positions below, each holding acts of its own kind.
- Nothing directly: an act is filed at the position that names it.

## Does not hold

- a standing surface rather than an act — a site, an account, an exposed interface, a repository page. These persist, are maintained, and decay; no position in the current topology holds them.
- the writing as produced work — `L8/L4/work/writing/`
- the research meaning, argument, or claim — `L8/L5/research/`
- the internal history of drafts, versions, and supersessions — `L8/L6/history/`; retired states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- the provenance of what went out — `L7/provenance/`
- the copyright, licence, permission, access, or controlled conditions on it — `L7/governance/`
- the binding to a named peer — `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
