# L7/allications/

**Object:** Directory for the bounded object's outward acts: publications, applications, communications, specifications, notifications, and participations.
**Path:** `L7/allications/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The owner named this position on 2026-09-19, quoted as written: "└── controlled/0 changed to move all cation words into "allications" (all-ications), pronounced ally-cations." It is one of the four canonical direct L7 branches.

What the six share is not a spelling. Each names a way the bounded object acts or participates outwardly: publishing, applying, communicating, specifying, notifying, or taking part. Some are discrete events; others, especially participation, may extend across a period. The name is a mnemonic for that family, not the test for membership.

## Why it exists

The bounded object can meet the outside world through more than publication. This branch keeps several externally situated acts or engagements separately addressable so publication, application, communication, specification, notification, and participation are not flattened into one generic "external" event.

- Nothing directly: an act is filed at the position that names it.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
