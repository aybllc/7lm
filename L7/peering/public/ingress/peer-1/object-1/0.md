## What this directory is

Directory identifying one bounded peer-owned or peer-directed object. Here the object is peer-owned and entering under public exposure — "Public ingress is the natural peering position for externally owned, publicly available definitions or peer objects." — where exposure (public) and direction (ingress) are independent distinctions fixed by the parent directories. `object-1` is a canonical position name, not the name of any actual object. "The object may be a document, database, foreign/federated data endpoint, or repository." What is filed is the binding, not a copy: the exact version, tagged with "object identity, owner, source layer, version, and ingress binding". "A peer-owned ingress object is bound/reference-only: NO EDIT and NO DELETE by the receiving repository." The bound object "may be carried inward to receiving L0 and referenced at any needed layer with its immutable external-ownership tag intact", and "External ingress is not silently admitted to L6-L0." In the originating case the object at this position, seen from Auditonomous, is the exact version of the Autonomous L0 definition: "Receipt at L0 does not copy authorship."

## Why it exists

Allows one peer to expose or receive multiple objects without treating the peer itself as the object. Peer-owned ingress is reference/binding only: NO EDIT and NO DELETE by the receiving repository.

## Holds
- one binding to one bounded peer-owned object: exact version, immutable external-ownership tag, direction, and standing (authorized, denied, quarantined, or retired) "without deleting its history"

## Does not hold
- a substitute for the owner's object: "it never becomes a copied substitute or locally authored definition"
- source, version, authority, and prior-art relation — L7/provenance/
- copyright, licensing, permissions, and access conditions — L7/governance/
- the binding's retirement or supersession history — L8/L6/retired/
- interpretation of the object — L8/L5/; a locally authored definition — L8/L0/semantics/definitions/

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"To change an immutable external object, return to its owner. The repository using the object does not edit the peer-owned source." "An upstream change creates a new binding; it does not mutate the pinned object."
