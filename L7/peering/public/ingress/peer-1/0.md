## What this directory is

`peer-1/` distinguishes one external counterpart within the **public ingress** channel.

The parent directories already state exposure and direction. This position contributes counterpart identity only.

The peer may be a person, group, institution, repository, service, model, laboratory, publisher, standards body, or another identifiable external object. `peer-1` is a local enumerated position, not a rank, authority level, ownership claim, or scientific status.

Matching peer numbers in another peering channel do not by themselves assert identity.

## Why it exists

A public inbound surface may receive interactions from many external counterparts. Separating the peer from its objects keeps counterpart identity distinct from each particular interaction.

## Holds

- the local identity of one external counterpart in public ingress;

## Does not hold

- the object or interaction itself;
- source authority, provenance, acceptance, or scientific validity merely because the peer is named.
