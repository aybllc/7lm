# L7/peering/public/ingress/peer-1/

**Object:** Directory identifying the external peer within an ingress or egress position. `peer-1` "identifies the external peer".
**Path:** `L7/peering/public/ingress/peer-1/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

Directory identifying the external peer within an ingress or egress position. The position here is "Public inbound peering position." — exposure (public) and direction (ingress) are independent distinctions fixed by the parent directories, not by this one. `peer-1` is a canonical position name, not the name of any actual peer; a repository instantiating the position names the peer it identifies. The peer is the owner from which a bound object enters, and its identity is the "owner" element of the tag every inward use carries: "object identity, owner, source layer, version, and ingress binding". The peer itself stays outside the boundary; only its identity and its objects are represented. In the originating case, seen from Auditonomous, the peer at this position is Autonomous, which "owns the locally authored definition of Autonomous".

## Why it exists

Keeps peer identity distinct from the particular object exchanged with that peer.

## Holds
- the identity of one external peer at this position
- the objects that peer exposes, one per object-1/: "Allows one peer to expose or receive multiple objects without treating the peer itself as the object."

## Does not hold
- the bounded object itself — L7/peering/public/ingress/peer-1/object-1/
- represented source/owner authority — L7/provenance/authority/
- copyright, licensing, and permissions — L7/governance/
- the peer's semantics: "L7 owns the external relation, not the lower-layer scientific meaning."

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
