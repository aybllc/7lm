## What this directory is

Peering branch for public exposure or custody. This directory names one value of the exposure/custody distinction only; direction is named beneath it, at L7/peering/public/ingress/ and L7/peering/public/egress/. The two are independent: "Within peering, exposure/custody (public versus private) and direction (ingress versus egress) remain independent semantic distinctions." Public is the exchange side of the forward-face distinction "private custody <-> public exchange".

Public exposure transfers nothing. An object entering here enters "without transferring ownership"; an object leaving here is "public outward exchange toward a peer; the released publication object itself is held by L7/allications/publications/." A person filing here records a relation whose exposure is public — "Public ingress is the natural peering position for externally owned, publicly available definitions or peer objects" and "Public egress handles outward public exchange toward peers" — and passes the L7 acceptance checks: "Source, version, authority, and provenance are explicit where required." "Applicable public/legal governance is explicit where required." "External ingress is not silently admitted to L6-L0." "Publication or legal status does not strengthen scientific evidence."

## Why it exists

Keeps public exposure distinct from whether the object is entering or leaving. A public relation can run in either direction, and its publicity is a property of exposure, not of which way the object moves.

## Holds
- L7/peering/public/ingress/ — "Public inbound peering position."
- L7/peering/public/egress/ — "Public outbound peering position."
- beneath each, */peer-1/ and */peer-1/object-1/ for the peer and the bounded object exchanged under public exposure

## Does not hold
- non-public exposure or custody — L7/peering/private/
- the released publication object — L7/allications/publications/ ("Released publications are represented by L7/allications/publications/")
- copyright, licensing, and permissions that govern public use — L7/governance/
- source, version, and authority of a public object — L7/provenance/
- binding history — L8/L6/; retirement or supersession of a binding — L8/L6/retired/
- lower-layer scientific meaning: "Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored."

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."
