# L7/peering/public/

**Object:** Public-exposure peering channel.
**Path:** `L7/peering/public/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/
**Local question:** Which peering relationships are exposed through a public-facing channel?

## What this directory is

`public/` is the peering channel for external relationships whose exposure is public.

Public describes exposure, not direction and not scientific status. Direction is represented separately beneath this position by `ingress/` and `egress/`.

A public relation may involve a person, institution, repository, service, model, standards body, publisher, laboratory, or another external counterpart. The fact that the relation is public does not determine what kind of peer it is or what the relation means scientifically.

## Why it exists

Some external relationships are intentionally exposed to an open audience: a public repository issue, public inter-repository exchange, openly identified collaboration, public request or response, or another visible interaction.

This position separates **public exposure** from **direction**.

## Holds

- `ingress/` — public relations directed toward the bounded object;
- `egress/` — public relations directed toward an external counterpart;
- the fact that the peering relation is publicly exposed.

## Does not hold

- restricted exposure — `L7/peering/private/`;
- publication merely because something is public — released publications are represented by `L7/allications/publications/`;
- provenance, rights, permissions, or scientific validity;
- a requirement that every public source be represented as a peer.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Public is an exposure coordinate only. Public exposure does not turn an interaction into publication, evidence, validation, adoption, or endorsement.

## Exemplar

An external researcher opens a public issue on a research repository and the repository owner responds publicly. The issue is public ingress; the response is public egress. Neither interaction becomes a publication merely because it is publicly visible.

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty.
- Public exposure and direction are independent peering coordinates.
- Machine metadata may describe exposure in an implementation, but no such automation is instantiated here.
