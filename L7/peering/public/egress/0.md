**Local question:** What identifiable external relation is directed from the bounded object through a public channel?

## What this directory is

Public egress represents peering directed from the bounded object toward an external counterpart through a publicly exposed relationship.

[
	ext{bounded object}ightarrow	ext{external counterpart}
]

The outgoing object or interaction may be a public response, request, dataset, definition, message, artifact, service interaction, or another distinguishable representation.

Egress describes direction. Public describes exposure. Public egress is not identical to publication.

## Why it exists

A bounded object may interact outward with an identifiable external counterpart in public without the interaction itself being a released publication. This position keeps the peer relation distinct from the publication act.

## Holds

- `peer-N/` positions for external counterparts addressed through this channel;
- `object-M/` positions for distinguished outgoing objects or interactions;
- the combined coordinates **public + egress**.

## Does not hold

- public inbound relations — `L7/peering/public/ingress/`;
- restricted outbound relations — `L7/peering/private/egress/`;
- a released publication merely because the exchange is public — `L7/allications/publications/` represents publication;
- provenance, governance, or scientific validity.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Public exposure does not convert a peering interaction into a publication, endorsement, or scientific conclusion.

## Exemplar

A research repository publicly responds to a named external repository or researcher. That response is public egress. A separately released article remains a publication even if no specific peer is addressed.

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty.
- Public egress and publication are separately representable external facts.
