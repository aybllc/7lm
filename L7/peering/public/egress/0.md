# L7/peering/public/egress/

**Object:** Public outbound peering position.
**Path:** `L7/peering/public/egress/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

Public outbound peering position. Exposure (public) is fixed by the parent L7/peering/public/; direction (egress) is fixed here; the two are independent distinctions. "Public egress handles outward public exchange toward peers; private egress supports restricted outbound exchange." What is filed is a relation under which a bounded object is exposed publicly toward a peer: the peer at peer-1/, the peer-directed object at peer-1/object-1/.

The L7 Directional inputs row for outward movement reads "Outward release: approved L6-backed packages."; this position is not the released publication object, which is held by L7/publications/. A person filing here records the exact version exposed and the peer it is directed to. This is the source side of the originating case: "Autonomous owns the locally authored definition of Autonomous." "Autonomous L7 public egress may expose an exact version of that Autonomous L0 definition." The route is "Source L0 definition → source L7 egress → receiving L7 ingress → receiving L0." and "A source object's L0 definition is an L7 egress object at its boundary; to us it is L7 ingress directed to our L0. Its semantic identity remains source-owned L0 throughout the exchange." Exposure adds nothing to the object: "Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."

## Why it exists

Represents public outward exchange toward a peer; the released publication object itself is held by the L7 publications branch. Egress is a relation to a peer; a publication is a released object, and the two are separately addressable.

## Holds
- peer-1/ — the external peer toward which public exchange is directed
- peer-1/object-1/ — one bounded peer-directed object, the exact version exposed to that peer
- the binding's standing: authorized, denied, quarantined, or retired, "without deleting its history"

## Does not hold
- restricted outbound exchange — L7/peering/private/egress/
- inbound material — L7/peering/public/ingress/
- the released publication object — L7/publications/
- copyright, licensing, and permissions governing public use — L7/governance/
- the definition being exposed, which lives once at its owner — L8/L0/semantics/definitions/ when this repository is the author
- the package's internal memory — L8/L6/

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Autonomous freezes the narrowest reasonable agreement among its exact source-tagged standards inputs as the Autonomous consensus baseline. It must not manufacture consensus where sources materially disagree." "An upstream change creates a new binding; it does not mutate the pinned object. A change to the Autonomous definition returns to the Autonomous owner."
