## What this directory is

Directory identifying one bounded peer-owned or peer-directed object. Here the object is peer-directed and leaving under public exposure — "Public egress handles outward public exchange toward peers" — where exposure (public) and direction (egress) are independent distinctions fixed by the parent directories. `object-1` is a canonical position name, not the name of any actual object. "The object may be a document, database, foreign/federated data endpoint, or repository." What is filed is the exact version directed to the peer and the relation under which it leaves; the L7 Directional inputs row for outward movement reads "Outward release: approved L6-backed packages."; this position is not the released publication object. Leaving is not release and adds nothing to the object: "the released publication object itself is held by the L7 publications branch", and "Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed." In the originating case the object at this position, seen from Autonomous, is "an exact version of that Autonomous L0 definition".

## Why it exists

Allows one peer to expose or receive multiple objects without treating the peer itself as the object. Peer-owned ingress is reference/binding only: NO EDIT and NO DELETE by the receiving repository.

## Holds
- one relation for one bounded peer-directed object: exact version exposed, the peer it is directed to, direction, and standing (authorized, denied, quarantined, or retired) "without deleting its history"

## Does not hold
- the object's owning representation, which lives once at the interior layer that owns it — L8/L0/semantics/definitions/ when it is a locally authored definition
- copyright, licensing, permissions, and access conditions on the exchange — L7/governance/
- source, version, and authority as represented outward — L7/provenance/
- the package's internal memory and the relation's binding history — L8/L6/
- the released publication object — L7/publications/

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"An upstream change creates a new binding; it does not mutate the pinned object. A change to the Autonomous definition returns to the Autonomous owner. Auditonomous updates or retires its binding; it does not edit the peer-owned definition."
