# L7/peering/private/

**Object:** Peering branch for non-public exposure or custody.
**Path:** `L7/peering/private/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

Peering branch for non-public exposure or custody. This directory names one value of the exposure/custody distinction only; direction is named beneath it, at L7/peering/private/ingress/ and L7/peering/private/egress/. The two are independent: "Within peering, exposure/custody (public versus private) and direction (ingress versus egress) remain independent semantic distinctions." Private is the custody side of the forward-face distinction "private custody <-> public exchange".

Private does not mean internally owned. What enters here enters "from a peer without making it public or internally owned"; what leaves here leaves "toward a peer without making it a public release". A person filing here records a relation whose exposure is non-public — "Private ingress is available for controlled inbound material" and "private egress supports restricted outbound exchange" — and passes the L7 acceptance checks: "Source, version, authority, and provenance are explicit where required." "Applicable public/legal governance is explicit where required." "External ingress is not silently admitted to L6-L0." Access conditions and restrictions applicable to the object are recorded at L7/governance/access/ and L7/governance/controlled/, not here.

## Why it exists

Keeps exposure/custody distinct from direction. A private relation can run in either direction, and its privacy is a property of custody, not of which way the object moves.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"No outsider traverses the USO by default. What an outside reader or system needs is represented at the forward face."
