# L7/peering/private/

**Object:** Restricted-exposure peering channel.
**Path:** `L7/peering/private/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/
**Local question:** Which peering relationships are exposed only to a restricted audience?

## What this directory is

`private/` is the peering channel for external relationships whose exposure is restricted.

Private describes **who can inspect the represented relationship**, not the direction of the relationship and not the scientific status of anything carried through it. Direction is represented separately beneath this position by `ingress/` and `egress/`.

A private peering relation remains an L7 surface relation. It is not hidden from the architecture merely because its audience is limited.

Here **auditable** means that the relation can be identified and inspected by the audience to which the channel is exposed. The channel does not prescribe who that audience must be or what audit procedure must be used.

## Why it exists

The bounded object may have externally accountable relationships that are not publicly exposed: teacher feedback, journal review, institutional collaboration, restricted service interaction, private data exchange, or another limited relationship.

This position separates **restricted exposure** from **direction**.

## Holds

- `ingress/` — restricted-exposure relations directed toward the bounded object;
- `egress/` — restricted-exposure relations directed toward an external counterpart;
- the fact that the peering relation is exposed to a limited rather than public audience.

## Does not hold

- public exposure — `L7/peering/public/`;
- direction by itself — stated by `ingress/` or `egress/`;
- permissions, access rights, legal restrictions, or disclosure rules — `L7/governance/` when those questions matter;
- scientific meaning or validity;
- a requirement that private material be secret, immutable, accepted, or retained in any particular way.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Private is an exposure coordinate only. A relationship can be private and incoming, private and outgoing, or represented in both directions through separate interactions.

Private exposure does not make an external object internal science.

## Exemplar

A student sends a draft to a teacher and receives comments through a course system visible only to them and authorized course participants. Both directions are private peering even though one is egress and the other ingress.

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty.
- Restricted exposure does not imply unauditability; it limits the audience of inspection.
- Machine metadata may describe exposure in an implementation, but no such automation is instantiated here.
