**Path:** `L7/peering/private/egress/peer-1/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

Directory identifying the external peer within an ingress or egress position. The position here is "Private outbound peering position." — exposure (private) and direction (egress) are independent distinctions fixed by the parent directories, not by this one. `peer-1` is a canonical position name, not the name of any actual peer; a repository instantiating the position names the peer it identifies. The peer is the recipient toward which a peer-directed object leaves under restricted exchange. The peer itself stays outside the boundary; only its identity and the objects directed to it are represented.

## Why it exists

Keeps peer identity distinct from the particular object exchanged with that peer.

## Holds
- the identity of one external peer at this position
- the objects directed to that peer, one per object-1/: "Allows one peer to expose or receive multiple objects without treating the peer itself as the object."

## Does not hold
- the bounded object itself — L7/peering/private/egress/peer-1/object-1/
- represented source/owner authority — L7/provenance/authority/
- restrictions and permissions on the exchange — L7/governance/controlled/, L7/governance/permissions/
- the object's semantics: "L7 owns the external relation, not the lower-layer scientific meaning."

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
