# L7/peering/private/egress/

**Object:** Private outbound peering position.
**Path:** `L7/peering/private/egress/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

Private outbound peering position. Exposure (private) is fixed by the parent L7/peering/private/; direction (egress) is fixed here; the two are independent distinctions. "private egress supports restricted outbound exchange." What is filed is a relation under which a bounded object leaves toward a peer under non-public custody: the peer at peer-1/, the peer-directed object at peer-1/object-1/.

The L7 Directional inputs row for outward movement reads "Outward release: approved L6-backed packages."; this position is not a public release. A person filing here records what is directed to the peer, the exact version exposed, and the restriction that keeps the exchange non-public, with the conditions themselves recorded at L7/governance/. Leaving does not promote: "Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."

## Why it exists

Represents something leaving toward a peer without making it a public release. Restricted exchange is a peering relation; release is a publication relation.

## Holds
- peer-1/ — the external peer toward which restricted material leaves
- peer-1/object-1/ — one bounded peer-directed object, the exact version exposed to that peer
- the binding's standing: authorized, denied, quarantined, or retired, "without deleting its history"

## Does not hold
- public outbound exchange — L7/peering/public/egress/
- inbound material — L7/peering/private/ingress/
- restrictions, permissions, and access conditions that keep the exchange non-public — L7/governance/controlled/, L7/governance/permissions/, L7/governance/access/
- the released publication object — L7/allications/publications/
- the package's internal memory — L8/L6/
- the object's scientific meaning, which stays at the interior layer that owns it

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"An upstream change creates a new binding; it does not mutate the pinned object."
