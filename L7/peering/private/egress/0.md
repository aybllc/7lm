**Local question:** What identifiable external relation is directed from the bounded object through a restricted channel?

## What this directory is

Private egress represents peering directed from the bounded object toward an external counterpart while the relationship is exposed only to a restricted audience.

[
	ext{bounded object}ightarrow	ext{external counterpart}
]

The outgoing object or interaction may be a manuscript, question, request, dataset, message, response, artifact, service call, or another distinguishable representation.

Egress describes direction. Private describes exposure. Neither coordinate makes the interaction a publication or establishes scientific standing.

## Why it exists

A bounded object may send or expose something to a specific external counterpart without making that interaction public. This position represents that relationship.

## Holds

- `peer-N/` positions for external counterparts addressed through this channel;
- `object-M/` positions for distinguished outgoing objects or interactions;
- the combined coordinates **private + egress**.

## Does not hold

- private inbound relations — `L7/peering/private/ingress/`;
- public outbound relations — `L7/peering/public/egress/`;
- released publication merely because an object left the boundary;
- rights, permissions, provenance, or scientific validity.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Leaving the bounded object through private peering records an external relationship. It does not by itself mean release to the public or completion of the research.

## Exemplar

A manuscript submitted to a journal through a restricted editorial portal is private egress. The later publication, if one occurs, is a separate outward act.

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty.
- Egress does not prescribe a release or approval workflow.
