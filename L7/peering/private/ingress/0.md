**Object:** Restricted-exposure inbound peering position.

**Local question:** What identifiable external relation is directed toward the bounded object through a restricted channel?

## What this directory is

Private ingress represents peering directed from an external counterpart toward the bounded object while the relationship is exposed only to a restricted audience.

[
	ext{external counterpart}ightarrow	ext{bounded object}
]

The incoming object or interaction may be a message, dataset, review, request, result, definition, artifact, service response, or another distinguishable representation.

Ingress describes direction. Private describes exposure. Neither coordinate states what the bounded object must do with what arrives.

## Why it exists

A bounded object may receive externally directed material or interaction through channels that are not public. This position identifies that relation without converting receipt into acceptance, adoption, trust, authorship, or scientific validity.

## Holds

- `peer-N/` positions for external counterparts using this channel;
- `object-M/` positions for distinguished incoming objects or interactions;
- the combined coordinates **private + ingress**.

## Does not hold

- private outbound relations — `L7/peering/private/egress/`;
- public inbound relations — `L7/peering/public/ingress/`;
- acceptance, review disposition, scientific interpretation, provenance, or governance merely because something arrived.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Receipt is a surface event. Any later scientific use belongs wherever that later representation can be stated correctly.

## Exemplar

Reviewer comments returned through a journal's restricted review system are private ingress. Whether the researcher accepts, rejects, answers, or ignores a comment is a different question.
