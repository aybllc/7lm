## What this directory is

Private inbound peering position. Exposure (private) is fixed by the parent L7/peering/private/; direction (ingress) is fixed here; the two are independent distinctions. "Private ingress is available for controlled inbound material." What is filed is a binding to a peer object entering under non-public custody: the peer at peer-1/, the bounded object at peer-1/object-1/.

The L7 directional-input rule governs entry: "Ingress: peer objects, source/authority information, and governance conditions initiate inward inspection and may carry an exact source-owned L0 definition to the receiving L0 without transferring authorship." A person filing here binds the exact version — "A peer-owned ingress object is bound/reference-only: NO EDIT and NO DELETE by the receiving repository." — and records source, version, authority and applicable public/legal/access conditions at L7/provenance/ and L7/governance/. "External ingress is not silently admitted to L6-L0."

## Why it exists

Represents something entering from a peer without making it public or internally owned. Privacy of custody and non-ownership are separate facts; this position records both.

## Holds
- peer-1/ — the external peer from which controlled material enters
- peer-1/object-1/ — one bounded peer-owned object, bound reference-only, with its immutable external-ownership tag ("object identity, owner, source layer, version, and ingress binding")
- the binding's standing: authorized, denied, quarantined, or retired, "without deleting its history"

## Does not hold
- public inbound material — L7/peering/public/ingress/
- outbound material — L7/peering/private/egress/
- access conditions, restrictions, and permissions — L7/governance/access/, L7/governance/controlled/, L7/governance/permissions/
- source, version, authority — L7/provenance/sources/, L7/provenance/authority/
- retirement or supersession of a binding — L8/L6/retired/
- a locally authored definition — L8/L0/semantics/definitions/

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"To change an immutable external object, return to its owner. The repository using the object does not edit the peer-owned source."
