## What this directory is

`peer-1/` distinguishes one external counterpart within the **private ingress** channel.

The parent directories already state exposure and direction. This position contributes counterpart identity only.

The peer may be a person, group, institution, repository, service, model, laboratory, publisher, instrument, or another identifiable external object. `peer-1` is a local enumerated position, not a claim about authority, rank, ownership, or scientific standing.

Matching peer numbers in another peering channel do not by themselves assert identity. A repository may state cross-channel identity separately when that distinction matters.

## Why it exists

One channel may involve many external counterparts, and one counterpart may participate in many interactions. Separating `peer-N/` from `object-M/` keeps counterpart identity distinct from the particular object or interaction.

## Holds

- the local identity of one external counterpart in private ingress;
- `object-M/` positions for interactions associated with that counterpart.

## Does not hold

- the object or interaction itself — represented beneath `object-M/`;
- authority, provenance, permissions, acceptance, or scientific meaning merely because the peer is identified.

## Exemplar

In a restricted journal review system, the editorial system or reviewer may be the locally identified peer. The returned review is represented beneath an object position.
