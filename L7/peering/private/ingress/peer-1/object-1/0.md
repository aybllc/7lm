## What this directory is

`object-1/` distinguishes one object or interaction within the relationship already located by its parent path:

- exposure: private;
- direction: ingress;
- counterpart: peer-1.

The object may be a document, dataset, message, definition, review, request, response, result, software artifact, endpoint reference, physical-artifact record, or another bounded representation.

The identifier distinguishes the instance. It does not by itself state who owns it, who authored it, whether it is immutable, whether it is accepted, what version it is, or whether it is scientifically valid.

## Why it exists

One peer can direct multiple distinct things toward the bounded object. `object-M/` lets those interactions remain distinguishable without turning the peer itself into the object.
