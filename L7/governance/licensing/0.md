# L7/governance/licensing/

**Object:** Directory for licensing terms or status when applicable.
**Path:** `L7/governance/licensing/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for licensing terms or status when applicable." A record here states the licence under which an external object was received, or the licence under which a local object is released: the grantor, the terms, the status, and what use the terms permit. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, controlled").

A record here describes the licence relation as it stands for the bounded object: the licence or licensing status, issuing party where relevant, scope, and terms that matter to the represented external relation. It does not itself impose a universal intake, quarantine, retirement, or repository-handling workflow.

## Why it exists

"Keeps permissions of use explicit at the external face." A licence is a condition an outside reader or a receiving peer must be able to see before using an object; it is not a fact about the object's science.

## Holds

- Licensing terms or status associated with externally relevant objects.
- Licensing terms or status associated with objects released through `L7/allications/publications/` or involved in a peer relation where applicable.
- The scope, issuer, duration, or use conditions of a licence when those properties matter to the bounded object.

## Does not hold

- the licensed object itself merely because a licence is represented here; the object's own representation remains wherever its role places it
- copyright ownership as such — `L7/governance/copyright/`
- explicit permissions granted for a particular use, disclosure, or exchange — `L7/governance/permissions/`
- restrictions that bar disclosure, transfer, or handling — `L7/governance/controlled/`
- the source's identity, version, and issuer — `L7/provenance/sources/` and `L7/provenance/authority/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Publication or legal status does not strengthen scientific evidence."
