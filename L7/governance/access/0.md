# L7/governance/access/

**Object:** Directory for access conditions applicable to externally facing objects.
**Path:** `L7/governance/access/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for access conditions applicable to externally facing objects." A record here states who may access an externally facing object and under what condition: public, paywalled, institutionally licensed, sign-in gated, private, or embargoed. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, controlled").

A person filing here records the access condition as it stands at the boundary, for both directions: the condition under which an external source could be obtained (or could not), and the condition under which a local object is exposed. Access is a governance condition. Exposure/custody (public versus private) is a peering distinction recorded at `L7/peering/`; the spec states that "Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions."

## Why it exists

"Makes who may access an object a boundary condition rather than an internal scientific claim." An access condition is what an outside reader or system meets first; it says nothing about the object's content.

## Holds

- Access conditions on externally facing objects: who may reach them, by what route, and under what gate.
- Access conditions met when obtaining external sources, including the record that a source was not obtained because its access condition was not met.
- The access condition attached to a publication or egress object.

## Does not hold

- the source or object itself — `L7/peering/` (ingress, NO EDIT and NO DELETE) or `L7/allications/publications/`
- whether the object is publicly or privately held — `L7/peering/public/` and `L7/peering/private/`
- licence terms that follow from the access route — `L7/governance/licensing/`
- restrictions on onward disclosure — `L7/governance/controlled/`
- the identity and version of the source obtained — `L7/provenance/sources/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"No outsider traverses the USO by default. What an outside reader or system needs is represented at the forward face."
