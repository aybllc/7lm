# L7/governance/restrictions/

**Object:** Directory for restrictions that constrain external disclosure, use, transfer, or handling.
**Path:** `L7/governance/restrictions/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for restrictions that constrain external disclosure, use, transfer, or handling." A record here states what may not be done with an object at the boundary: not redistributed, not reproduced at length, not used on-line, not transferred, not disclosed, not opened. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, restrictions"), and "apply public/legal permissions and restrictions" is a permitted L7 action.

A person filing here records the restriction, its source (a licence, a copyright holder, a patent filing, an owner ruling), and the objects it binds. A restriction may be the reason a binding is denied, quarantined, or retired; the permitted action is to "authorize, deny, quarantine, retire a binding without deleting its history," so the restriction stays recorded here and the decision's history stays at `USO/l6/`.

## Why it exists

"Keeps restrictions explicit and machine-addressable without redefining the underlying research." A restriction constrains handling at the desk; it does not change what the object means or what the research concludes.

## Holds

- Restrictions on external disclosure, use, transfer, or handling of received or exposed objects.
- Restrictions that follow from a copyright, licence, patent, permission, or access condition recorded in the sibling governance positions, cross-referenced by path.
- Owner rulings that restrict what crosses outward, for example a ruling that a repository or object is not released.

## Does not hold

- the restricted object itself — `L7/peering/` or `L7/publications/`
- the copyright, licence, or patent condition from which the restriction derives — `L7/governance/copyright/`, `L7/governance/licensing/`, `L7/governance/patents/`
- who may access the object — `L7/governance/access/`
- what is affirmatively permitted — `L7/governance/permissions/`
- the history of a quarantine or retirement decision — `USO/l6/history/`; the retired binding — `USO/l6/retired/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."

## Standing
