# L7/governance/

**Object:** Directory for public/legal/custodial conditions attached to externally exposed or received objects.
**Path:** `L7/governance/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This is the governance branch of the forward face. The specification's prose cell: "Directory for public/legal/custodial conditions attached to externally exposed or received objects." It is divided into six canonical positions — `copyright/`, `licensing/`, `patents/`, `permissions/`, `access/`, and `controlled/` — and the layer "owns / represents" exactly that set: "public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, controlled."

A governance record describes an external condition that bears on the bounded object or something with which it interacts: copyright, licence, patent status, permission, access, or another controlled condition. Governance states the condition; it does not by itself prescribe a repository workflow, determine scientific validity, or imply that the object also participates in peering.

## Why it exists

"Separates legal and access conditions from provenance, peering direction, publication, and scientific judgment." Each of those answers a different question; the specification's forward-face model states that "Provenance, custody, authority, copyright, patent status, permissions, internal consistency, and publication each answer different questions; none of them alone establishes scientific truth."

## Holds

- Public, legal, access, and handling conditions relevant to the bounded object's external surface, each placed in the child directory that states that kind of condition.
- Governance conditions associated with a source, publication, peer interaction, or other external object when such conditions actually exist.
- The distinction among copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, and controlled conditions rather than collapsing them into one status.

## Does not hold

- the source, version, authority, or prior-art relation of the object — `L7/provenance/`
- the peer relationship itself when one exists — `L7/peering/`
- the released publication object — `L7/allications/publications/`
- the internal history of how a governance condition was interpreted, changed, or ceased to apply — `L8/L6/` when that history is retained
- any scientific judgment about the object — `L8/L5/research/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored." "Publication or legal status does not strengthen scientific evidence."

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty. Presence in the agnostic topology establishes admissible capability, not mandatory population; unused now does not mean removed. The specification's author note: "A classroom experiment may leave governance and publication branches empty; a science fair, public release, publication, patent, or controlled exchange can populate the relevant forward-face positions without changing the architecture."
- The prior scaffold's `L7/corpus/`, `L7/private/`, and `L7/public/` positions were cut by the owner in commit c0f46e1, which created this directory empty; governance was not a named directory before that commit. No inherited term in the Harmonization Record is placed at this directory.
- Machine metadata: described at `L7/`; not instantiated in this repository. No automation. An implementation may make governance conditions machine-readable without changing their semantic ownership here.
