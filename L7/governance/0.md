# L7/governance/

**Object:** Directory for public/legal/custodial conditions attached to externally exposed or received objects.
**Path:** `L7/governance/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This is the governance branch of the forward face. The specification's prose cell: "Directory for public/legal/custodial conditions attached to externally exposed or received objects." It is divided into six canonical positions — `copyright/`, `licensing/`, `patents/`, `permissions/`, `access/`, and `restrictions/` — and the layer "owns / represents" exactly that set: "public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, restrictions."

In practice, a person filing here records a condition that attaches to an object crossing the boundary in either direction: what may be copied, licensed, claimed, permitted, accessed, or must be withheld. The permitted actions are to "apply public/legal permissions and restrictions" and to "authorize, deny, quarantine, retire a binding without deleting its history"; the acceptance check is that "Applicable public/legal governance is explicit where required." On the inward route, "L7 governance records applicable public/legal/access conditions" for a bound definition after provenance has recorded its source, version, and authority.

## Why it exists

"Separates legal and access conditions from provenance, peering direction, publication, and scientific judgment." Each of those answers a different question; the specification's forward-face model states that "Provenance, custody, authority, copyright, patent status, permissions, internal consistency, and publication each answer different questions; none of them alone establishes scientific truth."

## Holds

- Public, legal, and custodial conditions attached to externally exposed or received objects, each placed in the child directory that owns the condition.
- The governance side of every peering binding and every release: the conditions under which an ingress object was received and an egress object or publication may leave.
- Records of a binding being authorized, denied, quarantined, or retired, with its history retained.

## Does not hold

- the source, version, authority, or prior-art relation of the object — `L7/provenance/`
- the bound object itself or its peer identity — `L7/peering/`
- the released publication object — `L7/allications/publications/`
- the internal history of when a condition was applied or changed — `L8/L6/history/`; retired bindings — `L8/L6/retired/`
- any scientific judgment about the object — `L8/L5/research/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored." "Publication or legal status does not strengthen scientific evidence."

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty. Presence in the agnostic topology establishes admissible capability, not mandatory population; unused now does not mean removed. The specification's author note: "A classroom experiment may leave governance and publication branches empty; a science fair, public release, publication, patent, or controlled exchange can populate the relevant forward-face positions without changing the architecture."
- The prior scaffold's `L7/corpus/`, `L7/private/`, and `L7/public/` positions were cut by the owner in commit c0f46e1, which created this directory empty; governance was not a named directory before that commit. No inherited term in the Harmonization Record is placed at this directory.
- Machine metadata: described at `L7/`; not instantiated in this repository. No automation. The specification's machine-metadata row names "public/legal governance, visibility, ... permissions, and NO EDIT/NO DELETE constraints for peer-owned ingress" as things metadata may identify; none is enabled here.
