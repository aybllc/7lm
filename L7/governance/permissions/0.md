# L7/governance/permissions/

**Object:** Directory for explicit permissions attached to external use, disclosure, or exchange.
**Path:** `L7/governance/permissions/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for explicit permissions attached to external use, disclosure, or exchange." A record here states a permission that has been granted or declared: who may use, disclose, or exchange which object, in which direction, and on what condition. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, controlled"), and "apply public/legal permissions and restrictions" is a permitted L7 action.

A person filing here records the permission as granted, with its grantor and scope. The peer-object contract supplies the standing permission that applies to every peer-owned ingress object: it "is bound/reference-only: NO EDIT and NO DELETE by the receiving repository." A permission here does not stand in for ownership and does not settle validity.

## Why it exists

"Keeps permission separate from ownership and from scientific validity." Who may act on an object is a boundary condition; who owns it is provenance; whether it is sound is interior research.

## Holds

- Explicit permissions attached to external use, disclosure, or exchange of objects crossing the boundary in either direction.
- The declared permission conditions under which an egress object or publication may leave, and under which an ingress object was received.
- Custodial permissions governing the repository's own handling of the forward face, when the owner states them.

## Does not hold

- the object the permission concerns — `L7/peering/` or `L7/allications/publications/`
- ownership or represented authority — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- licence terms as such — `L7/governance/licensing/`
- who may access an object — `L7/governance/access/`
- what is barred — `L7/governance/controlled/`
- the history of permission changes — `L8/L6/history/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"To change an immutable external object, return to its owner. The repository using the object does not edit the peer-owned source."

## Standing
