# L7/governance/controlled/

**Object:** Directory for restrictions and other conditions that constrain external disclosure, use, transfer, or handling.
**Path:** `L7/governance/controlled/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for restrictions and other conditions that constrain external disclosure, use, transfer, or handling." A record here states what may not be done with an object at the boundary: not redistributed, not reproduced at length, not used on-line, not transferred, not disclosed, not opened. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, controlled"), and "apply public/legal permissions and restrictions" is a permitted L7 action.

A record here describes a restriction or controlled condition, its stated source where relevant, and the object or external relation to which it applies. The position states the restriction; it does not prescribe a universal denial, quarantine, retirement, deletion, or enforcement workflow.

## Why it exists

"Keeps what is controlled explicit and machine-addressable without redefining the underlying research." A restriction constrains handling at the desk; it does not change what the object means or what the research concludes.

## Holds

- Restrictions on external disclosure, use, transfer, or handling of received or exposed objects.
- Restrictions that follow from a copyright, licence, patent, permission, or access condition recorded in the sibling governance positions, cross-referenced by path.
- Locally declared external-handling restrictions when the bounded object actually has them.

## Does not hold

- the restricted object itself merely because a restriction is represented here; the object's own representation remains wherever its role places it
- the copyright, licence, or patent condition from which the restriction derives — `L7/governance/copyright/`, `L7/governance/licensing/`, `L7/governance/patents/`
- who may access the object — `L7/governance/access/`
- what is affirmatively permitted — `L7/governance/permissions/`
- the internal history of how a restriction was interpreted, changed, or ceased to apply — `L8/L6/` when that history is retained

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Material crossing outward may lose fidelity and may never gain scientific maturity merely by being exposed."
