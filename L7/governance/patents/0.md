# L7/governance/patents/

**Object:** Directory for patent-related representation when applicable.
**Path:** `L7/governance/patents/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for patent-related representation when applicable." A record here states the patent status or patent-related condition attached to an object at the boundary: a filing, a claim scope, a determination that material is withheld pending filing, or a determination that an object is excluded from a filing. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, controlled").

A person filing here records the status as a boundary fact. The specification's author note names the case: "a science fair, public release, publication, patent, or controlled exchange can populate the relevant forward-face positions without changing the architecture." The acceptance check is that "Applicable public/legal governance is explicit where required."

## Why it exists

"Provides an agnostic forward-face position for patent status or materials without requiring every project to use it." Most projects leave it empty; a project that files keeps the status here rather than inside the science it covers.

## Holds

- Patent-related representation: status, scope of what is claimed or withheld, and its relation to objects released or exchanged at the forward face.
- The patent status from which a restriction on disclosure derives; the restriction itself is recorded at `L7/governance/controlled/`.

## Does not hold

- the invention's scientific content, formal relations, or engineering realization — `L8/L2/`, `L8/L3/`, `L8/L4/`
- licences granted over patented material — `L7/governance/licensing/`
- restrictions on disclosure as such — `L7/governance/controlled/`
- prior art searched against a claim — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`
- any judgment that patent status strengthens a claim — no layer; "Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored."

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
