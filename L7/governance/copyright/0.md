# L7/governance/copyright/

**Object:** Directory for copyright-related representation when applicable.
**Path:** `L7/governance/copyright/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for copyright-related representation when applicable." A record here states the copyright condition attached to an object at the boundary: who holds copyright in an external object that has been received, what copyright is asserted over an object being released, and what that condition permits or bars. It is one of the six governance positions the layer owns ("public/legal governance: copyright, licensing, patents, permissions, access, restrictions").

A person filing here records the condition as a fact about external use. The permitted actions are to "apply public/legal permissions and restrictions" and to "authorize, deny, quarantine, retire a binding without deleting its history"; a copyright condition may therefore be the reason a binding is quarantined or a source is held cite-only, and that reason is recorded here while the history of the decision stays at `USO/l6/`.

## Why it exists

"Copyright governs external use of an artifact; it does not change the artifact's scientific meaning." The condition is explicit at the desk so that an outside reader knows what may be reproduced, and no interior layer has to carry it.

## Holds

- Copyright-related representation for received objects: the holder, the condition, and what reproduction it permits or bars.
- Copyright-related representation for released objects at `L7/publications/` or `L7/peering/*/egress/`.
- The copyright reason for a binding's quarantine or cite-only status, with its history preserved rather than deleted.

## Does not hold

- the copyrighted object itself when bound — `L7/peering/public/ingress/peer-1/object-1/` or the private position (NO EDIT, NO DELETE)
- licence terms granted over the object — `L7/governance/licensing/`
- who owns or speaks for the object as a matter of authority — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- the source's identity and version — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- the object's scientific meaning or the verdict on it — `USO/l0/` through `USO/l5/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored."
