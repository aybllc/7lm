# L7/governance/copyright/

**Object:** Directory for copyright-related representation when applicable.
**Path:** `L7/governance/copyright/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This position represents copyright-related facts or claims relevant to the bounded object's external surface: for example, a stated copyright holder, copyright notice, asserted status, jurisdictionally relevant status, or uncertainty about that status. It is one of the six governance positions and remains distinct from licensing, particular permissions, access, and handling restrictions.

A copyright record describes the copyright relation as represented by the bounded object. It does not itself decide what a researcher must do, grant a licence, create permission, or establish scientific validity.

## Why it exists

"Copyright governs external use of an artifact; it does not change the artifact's scientific meaning." The condition is explicit at the desk so that an outside reader knows what may be reproduced, and no interior layer has to carry it.

## Distinction from neighboring governance positions

Copyright, licence, permission, access, and restriction answer different questions.

- `copyright/` represents copyright status, claim, holder, notice, or uncertainty where relevant.
- `licensing/` represents licence terms or status.
- `permissions/` represents a specific permission or grant.
- `access/` represents conditions under which an object can be reached.
- `controlled/` represents restrictions on disclosure, use, transfer, or handling.

The same external object may have several of these relations at once. Each is represented in the position that states that relation; none is made a substitute for the others.

## Holds

- Copyright status, claim, holder, notice, or uncertainty associated with an externally relevant object.
- Copyright representation associated with a source, publication, peer interaction, or other external artifact where applicable.
- Cross-references to related licensing, permission, access, or controlled conditions without absorbing those conditions into copyright.

## Does not hold

- the copyrighted object itself merely because copyright is represented here; the object's own representation remains wherever its role places it
- licence terms or status — `L7/governance/licensing/`
- who owns or speaks for the object as a matter of authority — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- the source's identity and version — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- the object's scientific meaning or the verdict on it — `L8/L0/` through `L8/L5/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored."
