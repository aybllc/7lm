## The 0.md gate convention

`0.md` is the local gate for its directory. It states what the directory means, what belongs
there, what does not, and the manual steps for working there. `0.md` files are not duplicate ledgers and carry no approval chains.

## Boundary

- This repository holds no automation. No scripts, workflows, actions, hooks, generators,
  schemas, or templates. Everything here is done by a person, by hand, and reviewed by
  reading.
- Custody establishes nothing scientific. Holding an artifact here, hashing it, pinning it,
  or tracing its provenance does not make it valid. Integrity, authority, internal
  consistency, and provenance are separate from scientific validity, and none of them
  substitutes for it.
- Mapping and similarity never establish identity or semantic authority, and formal
  consistency under one interpretation does not choose that interpretation.




L3: engineering mathematics
L3 contains mathematics as used in realization:
    • engineering mathematics;

Formal objects may already exist below this layer. L3 makes them usable under engineering conditions.

 L1: state space
L1 declares what can be represented.
It contains:
    • admissible states; 
    • domains; 
    • ranges; 
    • relations; 
    • invariants; 
    • exclusions; 
    • base logic; 
    • exact static structure. 
L1 is the fixed board on which formal mathematics can subsequently operate.


 L0: the deepest origin
L0 is the final and deepest directory encountered when walking inward.
It contains the origin conditions required before any state space or mathematics can be interpreted:
epistemic
ontology
semantics

L0 answers:
    • What is known or knowable? 
    • What kind of object is this? 
    • What does the object, term, symbol, or relation mean? 
    
    
So the inward path terminates at:
epistemic status+ontology+semantics​
