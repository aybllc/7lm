## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for source identity and exact source/version references." A record here identifies an external source precisely enough that the same object at the same version can be named again: source, locator, issuer, exact version, retrieval, declared scope, and the local artifact or layer it bears on. The layer owns "provenance: prior art, sources, lineage, authority" and accepts a record when "Source, version, authority, and provenance are explicit where required."

The local table is `L7/provenance/sources/source-ledger.md`. It was relocated in this pass from `USO/l6/provenance/` and is header-only: it carries no rows. Its eight column headings are the fields a row carries:

> Source ID | Source and locator | Issuer / owner | Exact version | Retrieved | Declared scope | Relevant artifact or layer | Status / notes

Its header sentence states the limit the specification also states: "Inclusion records provenance and does not by itself endorse a source or claim."

## Exemplar — curated source archive

One implementation may keep source files in a separate curated archive and place only source references here. Another may cite a publisher, DOI, library record, database identifier, local file, public URL, or another stable locator. 7LM does not require one storage architecture.

If a curated archive is also an identifiable external counterpart whose relationship to the bounded object matters, that relationship may additionally be represented through `L7/peering/`. If only the source relation matters, provenance alone is sufficient.

The current research program uses an external curated archive as one such implementation exemplar. Its operational rules belong to that implementation and do not define this directory.

## Why it exists

"Keeps source identification inspectable at the external face." A source is something an outside reader must be able to check, so its identity sits at the desk rather than inside internal memory.

## Holds

- `source-ledger.md` — one row per exact source/version reference (the ledger's `Exact version` column identifies which version a row names).
- Exact source/version references where exact version identity matters to the bounded object, whether or not the source also participates in peering.

## Does not hold

- the scientific meaning drawn from the source — that belongs to the interior layer where the meaning or claim functions; physical storage of source material is an implementation choice
- who owns or speaks for the source — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- what the source says in comparison with local work — `L8/L5/research/`
- copyright, licence, access, or restriction conditions on the source — `L7/governance/`
- the internal history of when the bounded object began, changed, or stopped using a source — `L8/L6/` when retained

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

A row in the ledger does not make its source valid, and "Publication or legal status does not strengthen scientific evidence."
