# L7/provenance/lineage/

**Object:** Directory for externally relevant lineage and derivation relationships.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/lineage/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This position represents externally relevant lineage among objects: derivation, descent, transfer, composition, revision, or another named provenance relation.

A lineage record identifies the related objects and the relation being asserted. It answers how externally relevant objects are connected in origin or development; it does not by itself establish that the derivation is valid, complete, or scientifically sound.

## Why it exists

"Makes origin and relation traceable without turning lineage into scientific validity." A traceable origin is a boundary fact an outside reader can check; it is kept separate from any lower-layer claim that the derived object is correct.

## Holds

- Externally relevant lineage records: derivation, descent, transfer, composition, revision, or other named origin relations.
- Relations among externally distinguishable versions or artifacts when those relations matter to provenance.
- Lineage associated with peer objects when a peering relationship also exists, without making peering a prerequisite for lineage.

## Does not hold

- the internal chronology of this repository's own work — `L8/L6/`
- retired or superseded local states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- exact source identity and version — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- prior art as antecedent landscape — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`
- who owns or speaks for the related objects — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- any judgment that a derivation is valid — `L8/L5/research/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

Git history may supply evidence for a lineage relation, but repository change history and provenance lineage are not identical objects. A lineage relation is represented here when the external relation itself matters.
