# L7/provenance/lineage/

**Object:** Directory for externally relevant lineage and derivation relationships.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/lineage/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for externally relevant lineage and derivation relationships." A record here states that one external object derives from, descends from, was transferred from, or was composed out of another — the relation itself, named and dated. It is one of the four provenance positions the layer owns ("provenance: prior art, sources, lineage, authority").

A person filing here names both ends of the relation and the relation type. The record answers "where did this come from and what is it related to"; it does not answer whether the derivation is sound. The acceptance check is that "Source, version, authority, and provenance are explicit where required."

## Why it exists

"Makes origin and relation traceable without turning lineage into scientific validity." A traceable origin is a boundary fact an outside reader can check; it is kept separate from any lower-layer claim that the derived object is correct.

## Holds

- Externally relevant lineage records: transfers of an object between owners or repositories, derivations of one external object from another, and the relation of a bound peer object to its upstream versions.
- The externally relevant relation between versions of a peer-owned object (the upstream version an existing binding points to and the upstream version a new binding points to). The binding event itself and its supersession are binding history and stay at `L8/L6/history/` and `L8/L6/retired/` ("L6 preserves binding history").

## Does not hold

- the internal chronology of this repository's own work and bindings — `L8/L6/history/`
- retired or superseded local states — `L8/L6/retired/`
- exact source identity and version — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- prior art as antecedent landscape — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`
- who owns or speaks for the related objects — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- any judgment that a derivation is valid — `L8/L5/research/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

The Harmonization Record's supporting-evidence disposition applies: "Git history can support L6 reconstruction, but Git history and scientific memory/provenance are not the same object." A commit graph is not a lineage record; a lineage record here is written prose with explicit ends and relation.
