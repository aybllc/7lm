# L7/provenance/

**Object:** Directory for the external provenance of the bounded object: where it came from, what preceded it, what source/version is being used, and what authority is being represented.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This is the provenance branch of the forward face. The specification's prose cell: "Directory for the external provenance of the bounded object: where it came from, what preceded it, what source/version is being used, and what authority is being represented." It is divided into four canonical positions — `prior-art/`, `sources/`, `lineage/`, and `authority/` — each answering one of those four questions, and L7 "owns / represents" exactly that set: "provenance: prior art, sources, lineage, authority."

In practice, a person filing here records an external relation of the bounded object, not a claim about its truth. The permitted actions are to "record provenance and prior art" and to "receive, bind, attribute, disclose, present, publish, release, route"; the acceptance check is that "Source, version, authority, and provenance are explicit where required." The route for an externally owned definition passes through here: "L7 provenance records source, version, authority, and prior-art relation; L7 governance records applicable public/legal/access conditions; L6 preserves binding history; L5 compares and interprets it."

## Why it exists

"Places externally meaningful source and lineage information at the forward face rather than burying it inside internal memory." Provenance about external objects is something an outside reader or system needs, so it is represented at the desk rather than inside `USO/l6/`.

## Holds

- Records of where an externally owned object or source came from, at what version, and who speaks for it — one record per relation, placed in the child directory that owns the question.
- The provenance relation of any peer object bound at `L7/peering/` (source, version, authority, prior-art relation).
- The source ledger `L7/provenance/sources/source-ledger.md` (see `L7/provenance/sources/0.md`).

## Does not hold

- the bound peer object itself — `L7/peering/public/ingress/peer-1/object-1/` or the corresponding private position
- copyright, licensing, patent, permission, access, or restriction conditions — `L7/governance/`
- the internal chronology of how a binding was made, used, corrected, or superseded — `USO/l6/history/`; retired bindings — `USO/l6/retired/`
- comparison, interpretation, or verdict on a source — `USO/l5/research/`
- the locally authored meaning of the bounded object — `USO/l0/semantics/definitions/`
- released publication objects — `L7/publications/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

The hard boundary applies to every record here: "Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored."
