# L7/provenance/

**Object:** Directory for the external provenance of the bounded object: where it came from, what preceded it, what source/version is being used, and what authority is being represented.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This is the provenance branch of the forward face. The specification's prose cell: "Directory for the external provenance of the bounded object: where it came from, what preceded it, what source/version is being used, and what authority is being represented." It is divided into four canonical positions — `prior-art/`, `sources/`, `lineage/`, and `authority/` — each answering one of those four questions, and L7 "owns / represents" exactly that set: "provenance: prior art, sources, lineage, authority."

A provenance record describes an external source relation of the bounded object, not a claim about the source's truth. Provenance can apply to material found through a publication, book, archive, database, web search, standards collection, peer relationship, or another source path. Peering is only one possible external relationship and is not required for provenance.

## Why it exists

"Places externally meaningful source and lineage information at the forward face rather than burying it inside internal memory." Provenance about external objects is something an outside reader or system needs, so it is represented at the desk rather than inside `L8/L6/`.

## Holds

- Records of where an external source came from, including version or issuing context where those distinctions matter.
- Provenance associated with a peering interaction when the same object also participates in a peer relationship.
- Provenance for external sources that have no peering representation at all.
- The source ledger `L7/provenance/sources/source-ledger.md` (see `L7/provenance/sources/0.md`).

## Does not hold

- the peering relationship itself when one exists — `L7/peering/`
- copyright, licensing, patent, permission, access, or restriction conditions — `L7/governance/`
- the internal chronology of how the bounded object used, changed, or stopped using a source — `L8/L6/` when that history is retained
- comparison, interpretation, or verdict on a source — `L8/L5/research/`
- the semantic meaning used by the bounded object — `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- released publication objects — `L7/allications/publications/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

The hard boundary applies to every record here: "Presence, publication, copyright, patent status, custody, or provenance at L7 does not make an object valid, complete, true, or internally authored."
