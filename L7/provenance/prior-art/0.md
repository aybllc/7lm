# L7/provenance/prior-art/

**Object:** Directory for identified prior art and antecedent work relevant to the bounded object.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/prior-art/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This position represents identified antecedent or prior work and the external relation that makes it relevant to the bounded object. An antecedent may be a standard, paper, prior system, dataset, method, artifact, or earlier work.

The prior-art position states **what the antecedent is and what relation is being represented**. It does not decide whether the antecedent defeats novelty, proves dependence, establishes correctness, or otherwise settles the research interpretation; those are L5 questions.

## Why it exists

"Makes the antecedent landscape explicit without confusing prior art with the object's locally authored L0 meaning." Under the prior scaffold, prior art sat inside L0; the harmonized specification moves it to the forward face so that an antecedent is never mistaken for a locally authored distinction.

## Holds

- Identified prior art and antecedent work, each with explicit source, version, and the relation it bears to the bounded object.
- Prior-art relations for external sources or objects, whether or not they also participate in a peering relationship.
- Existing prior-art research artifacts may be retained here as exemplars or unresolved filings without acquiring architectural authority. The current `uha-ommp-exemplar.html` is retained as such an artifact; its claims are not adjudicated in this 7LM architecture pass.

## Does not hold

- the L0 meaning used by the bounded object — `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- exact source/version identity as such — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- derivation relationships — `L7/provenance/lineage/`
- who owns or speaks for the antecedent — `L7/provenance/authority/`
- the comparison, interpretation, or judgment about whether the antecedent anticipates the local work — `L8/L5/research/`
- the antecedent artifact itself merely because its prior-art relation is represented here; its own representation remains wherever its role places it

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.
