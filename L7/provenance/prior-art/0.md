## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for identified prior art and antecedent work relevant to the bounded object." A record here identifies an antecedent — a standard, paper, prior system, or earlier work — and its relation to the bounded object. It is one of the four provenance positions the layer owns ("provenance: prior art, sources, lineage, authority"), and "record provenance and prior art" is a permitted L7 action.

A person filing here states what the antecedent is, at what version, and how it relates to the bounded object. The identification of the antecedent belongs here; what the local research concludes from the comparison does not. The acceptance check is that "Source, version, authority, and provenance are explicit where required."

## Why it exists

"Makes the antecedent landscape explicit without confusing prior art with the object's locally authored L0 meaning." Under the prior scaffold, prior art sat inside L0; the harmonized specification moves it to the forward face so that an antecedent is never mistaken for a locally authored distinction.
