# L7/provenance/authority/

**Object:** Directory for represented source/owner authority associated with the external object or record.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/authority/` · **Position:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

This position represents who is identified as issuing, authoring, owning, maintaining, publishing, certifying, or otherwise speaking for an external source or object, and in what stated capacity.

Authority here is a provenance relation. Recording it describes the source context; it does not transfer that authority to the bounded object and does not establish that the source is scientifically correct.

## Why it exists

"Distinguishes who owns or speaks for an external object from what the local research concludes about it." Authority about meaning stays with the owner; conclusions about the object stay with `L8/L5/`.

## Holds

- Represented issuing, authorship, ownership, maintenance, publication, certification, or comparable source capacity.
- The stated scope or capacity in which an external body or person speaks for an object.
- Multiple represented authorities where more than one external source has a relevant authority relation.

## Does not hold

- the external object itself merely because an authority relation is represented here
- exact source/version identity — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- what the local research concludes about the object — `L8/L5/research/`
- the semantic definition used by the bounded object — `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- legal permission to use or disclose the object — `L7/governance/permissions/` and the other governance positions

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and allications — the outward acts, of which publication is one. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

Recording represented authority does not transfer that authority to the bounded object and does not convert source status into scientific validity.
