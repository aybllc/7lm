# L7/provenance/authority/

**Object:** Directory for represented source/owner authority associated with the external object or record.
**Path:** `L7/provenance/authority/` · **Layer:** L7 — SURFACE / FORWARD FACE · **Owner:** L7/

## What this directory is

The specification's prose cell: "Directory for represented source/owner authority associated with the external object or record." A record here states who owns or speaks for an external object — the standards body, publisher, author, or peer repository — and in what capacity. It is one of the four provenance positions the layer owns ("provenance: prior art, sources, lineage, authority"), and the layer's function is to provide this surface "without absorbing external authority or granting lower-layer validity."

A person filing here records represented authority as a fact about the external object. The declared semantic-deference rule shows how far that goes: "the receiver intentionally takes the owner's exact bound definition as authoritative for what the owner means and does not re-prove or locally redefine that meaning. This declared trust is not a claim that the definition is objectively true, not an endorsement of downstream claims, and not a transfer of authorship."

## Why it exists

"Distinguishes who owns or speaks for an external object from what the local research concludes about it." Authority about meaning stays with the owner; conclusions about the object stay with `L8/L5/`.

## Holds

- Represented source/owner authority for external objects and records: the owner, the source layer at the owner, and the capacity in which the owner speaks.
- The authority element of a binding's immutable external-ownership tag ("object identity, owner, source layer, version, and ingress binding").
- Where more than one external body speaks for a term, the represented authority of each is a separate external fact; the spec's Autonomous case states that disagreement among source-tagged standards inputs is kept visible: "It must not manufacture consensus where sources materially disagree; every disagreement remains source-tagged and visible."

## Does not hold

- the owner's object itself — `L7/peering/public/ingress/peer-1/object-1/` or the private position (NO EDIT, NO DELETE)
- exact source/version identity — `L7/provenance/sources/`
- what the local research concludes about the object — `L8/L5/research/`
- the locally authored definition of the bounded object — `L8/L0/semantics/definitions/`
- legal permission to use or disclose the object — `L7/governance/permissions/` and the other governance positions

## Boundary / traversal rule

L7 is the forward face outside the USO. It owns the external relations of the bounded object: provenance, governance, peering, and publication state. Public/private exposure and ingress/egress direction remain independent peering distinctions. L7 does not thereby acquire or change the lower-layer scientific meaning.

"Metadata does not transfer semantic authority." Recording an authority here does not make this repository a second authority for the object, and "No receiving layer thereby becomes the definition's author."
