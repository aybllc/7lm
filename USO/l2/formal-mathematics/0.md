## What this directory is

Directory for the exact formal system: symbols, types, models, derivations, and obligations. It is the single owner of the bounded thing's exact mathematics inside `USO/l2/`, and its three children — `relations/`, `operators/`, and `proofs/` — separate the relational structure, the lawful actions, and the warrant for exact claims from one another.

In practice, a person filing here states formal symbols and types, exact models and carriers, and the derivations and obligations that go with them, over the L1 state-space contract at `USO/l1/state-space/`. The permitted actions are the layer's: formalize, derive, prove, type-check, construct, test closure; declare domain/codomain/partiality; mark conjectural or unproved claims explicitly. The acceptance check is the layer's: every symbol binds to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility; every operation has declared domain/codomain or partiality; exact results are supported by proof/derivation or marked as unproved. An object that fails the first row is raised at `USO/l0/` or `USO/l1/`, not repaired here.

## Why it exists

Keeps formal mathematics grouped under one exact owner before engineering choices are introduced. Engineering choices are introduced at `USO/l3/`; the exact statement they act on is filed here first.

## Holds

- formal symbols and types
- exact models, carriers, and transition systems, stated over the L1 state-space contract
- axioms and definitions in their formal form, bound to L0 meaning
- exact formulas
- the grouping of relations (`relations/`), operators (`operators/`), and proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations (`proofs/`)
- the exact L2 formal contract handed to `USO/l3/`: behavior, invariants, formulas, and unresolved proof obligations

## Does not hold

- semantic and foundational meaning of the symbols — `USO/l0/semantics/` and `USO/l0/semantics/definitions/`
- admissible and excluded states, dimensions, invariants, identity conditions — `USO/l1/state-space/`
- approximation and discretization — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/approximation/`
- numerical uncertainty and error characterization — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/uncertainty/`
- engineering tolerance and admissible engineering bounds — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/tolerances/`
- computation as actually run, and any measurement or realized output — `USO/l4/work/computation/` and `USO/l4/work/`
- research interpretation of what a formal result means — `USO/l5/research/`
- superseded prior formal states and their history — `USO/l6/retired/` and `USO/l6/history/`
- externally owned formal results and their source binding — `L7/provenance/` and `L7/peering/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership. The layer's hard boundary applies to every file here: no engineering tolerance, approximation quality, physical realization, measurement, or empirical truth is claimed here.
