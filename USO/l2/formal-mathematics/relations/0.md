# USO/l2/formal-mathematics/relations/

## What this directory is

Directory for exact relations among admitted formal objects. A relation filed here holds between objects that `USO/l1/state-space/` admits and that `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/` has given formal symbols and types; it states what holds exactly, not what acts (that is `operators/`) and not why the claim is warranted (that is `proofs/`).

In practice, a person filing here writes the relation with its domain declared, states whether it is total or partial over the admitted objects, and binds each symbol to its L0 meaning and its L1 admissibility. A relation asserted without proof or derivation is filed as unproved, explicitly, and its obligation is recorded at `proofs/`. Nothing here carries a tolerance, a unit of measurement, or a numerical approximation.

## Why it exists

Keeps relational structure distinguishable from the operators that act and the proofs that warrant claims. Separating the three lets an inspector locate the first failing element — the relation, the operation, or the warrant — without reading the others.

## Holds

- exact relations among admitted formal objects, including exact formulas stated as relations
- declared domain and partiality for each relation
- the binding of each relation's symbols to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility

## Does not hold

- operators, functions, gates, constructors, and other lawful formal actions — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the admissible and excluded states the relations range over — `USO/l1/state-space/`
- the meaning of the related terms — `USO/l0/semantics/`
- approximate, tolerant, or applied forms of a relation — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/`
- a relation as computed or measured — `USO/l4/work/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership.
