# USO/l2/formal-mathematics/proofs/

**Object:** Directory for proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations.

## What this directory is

Directory for proofs, lemmas, derivations, and explicit unproved obligations. It holds the warrant for exact claims made in `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/`, `relations/`, and `operators/`, and — with equal standing — the explicit record of what has not been warranted. The layer's third acceptance row is the rule of this directory: exact results are supported by proof/derivation or marked as unproved.

In practice, a person filing here writes the proof, lemma, or derivation over the L1 state-space contract, with each symbol bound to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility, or files the obligation as unproved, explicitly. Marking conjectural or unproved claims explicitly is a permitted action, not a failure; an unresolved proof obligation is part of the exact L2 formal contract that `USO/l3/` receives. A claim that cannot be proved over the current L1 contract is not proved by widening that contract here; the widening is proposed at `USO/l1/`.

## Why it exists

Keeps warrant for exact claims inspectable and separate from the claim’s notation or later numerical realization. A reader can then ask of any exact claim whether its warrant exists, where it is, and what it still owes, without confusing the proof with the symbol or with a computed instance.

## Holds

- proofs, lemmas, and derivations of exact claims
- explicit unproved obligations, marked as unproved
- conjectural claims, marked as conjectural
- the unresolved proof obligations that the exact L2 formal contract hands to `USO/l3/`

## Does not hold

- the relations being proved — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- the operators whose properties are proved — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- the admissibility of the states a proof ranges over — `USO/l1/state-space/`
- a numerical check, simulation, or error bound offered in place of a proof — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/`
- a proof-checking or test run as executed and its recorded outputs or failures — `USO/l4/work/computation/`
- research judgment about the weight or meaning of a result — `USO/l5/research/review/` and `USO/l5/research/findings/`
- a superseded proof retained for inspection — `USO/l6/retired/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership. No engineering tolerance, approximation quality, physical realization, measurement, or empirical truth is claimed here.
