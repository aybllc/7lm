# USO/l2/

**Object:** Exact formal objects and lawful operation over L1.
**Path:** `USO/l2/` · **Layer:** L2 — FORMAL MATHEMATICS · **Owner:** USO/l2/ — this layer owns the exact formal representation of the bounded thing and the lawful operations defined over its L1 state space.

L2 is the layer of the scientific interior where exact formal objects are stated over the admissible states that `USO/l1/` has frozen. It asks the spec's core question: "What follows exactly when lawful operations are defined over L1?" Everything here is exact. The layer states notation, types, relations, operators, constructors, carriers, exact models, derivations, and proof obligations; it does not choose meaning (that is `USO/l0/`), it does not admit or exclude states (that is `USO/l1/`), and it does not approximate, tolerate, measure, or realize (those begin at `USO/l3/` and `USO/l4/`).

### Owns / represents
- formal symbols and types
- relations, functions, operators, gates, constructors, carriers, transition systems
- axioms, definitions, lemmas, theorems, derivations, exact formulas

### Permitted actions
- formalize, derive, prove, type-check, construct, test closure
- declare domain/codomain/partiality
- mark conjectural or unproved claims explicitly

### Acceptance / boundary check
- Every symbol binds to L0 meaning and L1 admissibility.
- Every operation has declared domain/codomain or partiality.
- Exact results are supported by proof/derivation or marked as unproved.

## How the layer is used

The three rows of the acceptance check are the whole test for filing here. A symbol that binds to no L0 meaning or to no admitted L1 state is not yet an L2 object; the dependency is raised at `USO/l0/` or `USO/l1/`, not repaired here. An operation without a declared domain, codomain, or partiality is incomplete. An exact result without a proof or derivation is filed as unproved, explicitly, and is carried in the contract as an unresolved proof obligation. What leaves this layer is the exact L2 formal contract that `USO/l3/` receives: behavior, invariants, formulas, and the unresolved proof obligations, stated without tolerance.

The interior stays physically flat: `USO/l2/` is a sibling of `USO/l1/` and `USO/l3/` under `USO/`. The spec's analytic views — the L3 / L2 / L1 technical-possibility bundle and the tighter L3 / L2 sub-bundle "when L1 is already pinned and not itself under question" — are "selectable analytic rails, not new containers and not a requirement that every inquiry traverse every layer." They do not nest this layer inside another.

Where a thing is examined rather than built, this layer owns the representation the spec assigns it in the OSI-origin inquiry table: "Exact formal relations, operators, proofs, or formal constraints used to state the architecture where such formalization is actually warranted." A contained architecture realized at `USO/l4/` is not remapped to this layer because a formal question about it arises here.

Canonical-tree rule, in this layer's words: every branch drawn in the directory structure above is an admissible capability of this layer, not a population requirement. `formal-mathematics/` and its three children may stand empty for as long as no exact object of the bounded thing has been stated; unused now does not mean removed.

## Direction

Construction enters from `USO/l1/` and leaves toward `USO/l3/`; inspection enters from `USO/l3/` and proceeds toward `USO/l1/`. These are the spec's two valid directions — scientific construction outward L0→L1→L2→L3→L4→L5→L6→L7 "only as far as established dependencies justify," and authorized external inspection inward L7→L6→L5→L4→L3→L2→L1→L0 — and "Description does not become prescription merely because the structure can be traversed."
