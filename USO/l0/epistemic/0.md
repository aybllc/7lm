# USO/l0/epistemic/

**Object:** Directory for the Epistemic rail content owned by L0.
**Path:** `USO/l0/epistemic/` · **Layer:** L0 — LAYER 0 · **Owner:** USO/l0/

## What this directory is

Directory for the Epistemic rail content owned by L0. The Epistemic rail is one of the four rails the L0 sheet names as this object's own boundaries. What is held here is the epistemic distinction the bounded object itself establishes before an admissible state is declared at `USO/l1/`.

In practice a person filing here records an epistemic distinction this object authored, binds it to this rail, and where meaning is ambiguous or unresolved states so rather than filling it by guess; a changed distinction is versioned, not rewritten in place. An epistemic distinction is not a research conclusion, an evidence relation, or a verdict on a claim: L0 does not own "experiment, or research conclusion," and those belong above.

## Why it exists

Keeps epistemic distinctions inside the rail that owns them. Without this directory the epistemic standing of the object's meaning would be supplied silently by semantics or ontology, which the acceptance check "No neighboring rail silently supplies another rail's local meaning" forbids.

## Holds

- Epistemic-rail distinctions locally authored by this bounded object.
- Statements of ambiguity or unresolved meaning, recorded as unresolved.
- Versioned prior epistemic distinctions where a distinction has changed.
- References to any externally owned epistemic input, carried as an exact named external semantic binding with object identity, owner, source layer, version, ingress binding, and immutable status.

## Does not hold

- Externally owned definitions copied inward — bound through `L7/peering/`, represented at `L7/provenance/`.
- Ontological, semantic, or universality distinctions — `USO/l0/ontology/`, `USO/l0/semantics/`, `USO/l0/universality/`.
- Admissible states — `USO/l1/state-space/`.
- Research judgment, review, or findings about what is known — `USO/l5/research/`.
- Reconstructable development history and corrections beyond the versioned distinction itself — `USO/l6/history/`; superseded distinctions no longer current — `USO/l6/retired/`.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L0/semantics contains the semantic representation authored by the bounded thing. An externally owned definition is not copied into L0 merely to populate the directory.

## Standing

- This branch may remain empty. Presence in the agnostic topology establishes admissible capability, not mandatory population; unused now does not mean removed. A rail may remain sparse or gate-only when no local representation has been authored.
- Machine metadata: described at `USO/l0/`; not instantiated in this repository. No automation.
