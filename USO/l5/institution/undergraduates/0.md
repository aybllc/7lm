# USO/l5/institution/undergraduates/

**Object:** Directory for undergraduate participant positions within the institutional setting.
**Path:** `USO/l5/institution/undergraduates/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** USO/l5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for undergraduate participant positions within the institutional setting. An undergraduate position is a participant role in the research and teaching conducted at L5, distinguished from peer positions and from the research-owner role. The position is filed here; any claim, analysis, or review an undergraduate participant produces is filed at `USO/l5/research/` under the same rules as any other L5 claim.

In practice a person filing here records the undergraduate position and the role it carries in the research setting; the layer owns review, audit, research governance, and teaching, and an undergraduate position is one participant position within that setting. An undergraduate participant's work is held to the same acceptance check as every other L5 claim: no broader than evidence/maturity, with methods, exclusions, uncertainty, and inferential limits disclosed.

## Why it exists

Keeps undergraduate participation distinct from peer and research-owner roles. The three roles are then separately readable in the record, and a claim is read with its author's position known.

## Holds

- undergraduate participant positions within the institutional setting
- the role an undergraduate position carries in research participation and teaching

## Does not hold

- teaching material as produced writing — `USO/l4/work/writing/`; its research meaning stays L5
- what `USO/l0/pedagogy/` owns at L0; that sheet states it
- claims, analyses, and findings an undergraduate contributes — `USO/l5/research/`
- peer positions — `USO/l5/institution/peers/`
- access relations and permissions — `L7/governance/`
