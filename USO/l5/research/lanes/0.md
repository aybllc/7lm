# USO/l5/research/lanes/

**Object:** Directory for distinct lines of inquiry within the research record.
**Path:** `USO/l5/research/lanes/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** USO/l5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for distinct lines of inquiry within the research record. Each lane is one question or investigative path — its research questions, hypotheses, rationale, analyses, and interpretations — kept apart from every other lane so that what one path concludes is not read as what another path concluded. A lane frames a question and interprets the evidence it gathers; it compares claim to evidence and states the claim's scope, limitations, and uncertainty.

In practice a person filing here opens one lane per line of inquiry, names the L4 work and evidence and the L3–L0 contracts the lane depends on, and keeps the lane's claims no broader than that evidence and maturity. A research claim or thesis statement is filed here (or in the corresponding L5 research artifact) as a claim of the lane that asserts it, with its dependencies cited. Review of a lane's claims is filed at `USO/l5/research/review/`; what the lane may responsibly state as a finding is filed at `USO/l5/research/findings/`.

## Why it exists

Prevents separate questions or investigative paths from being collapsed into one undifferentiated research stream. Each lane keeps its own question and its own claims; a claim is read as the claim of the lane that asserts it.

## Holds

- distinct lines of inquiry, one lane per question or investigative path
- research questions, hypotheses, rationale, analyses, interpretations belonging to a lane
- research claims and thesis statements, as claims of the lane that asserts them, with cited dependencies, limitations, and review status
- literature search and relevance judgment performed within a lane

## Does not hold

- review, criticism, audit, or qualification of a lane's claims — `USO/l5/research/review/`
- findings stated from the available work — `USO/l5/research/findings/`
- the work and evidence a lane interprets — `USO/l4/work/`
- proofs and exact relations a claim depends on — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/`
- locally authored definitions a claim's terms depend on — `USO/l0/semantics/definitions/`
- external antecedents (prior art) — `L7/provenance/prior-art/`
- superseded lanes and retired claims — `USO/l6/`

## The working position

A lane is also where an item goes when the owner takes it up from the catch-all `USO/l5/research/review/`, and it goes back there if set down again. Position is state; the two moves are recorded by the repository's history and nothing is deleted.

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 may interpret externally owned definitions, but interpretation does not transfer ownership of the peer definition into L0. Consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact.
