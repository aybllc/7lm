# USO/l5/research/

**Object:** Directory for the active research record and discourse owned at L5.
**Path:** `USO/l5/research/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** USO/l5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for the active research record and discourse owned at L5. It is where the layer's core question — "What may responsibly be concluded, questioned, taught, compared, or decided from the available work?" — is worked on the record: research questions, hypotheses, rationale, analyses, interpretations, and findings, together with literature search and relevance judgment and the claim scope, limitations, uncertainty, explanation, and conclusion that attach to each claim.

In practice a person filing here places a line of inquiry under `USO/l5/research/lanes/`, review of a claim under `USO/l5/research/review/`, and a finding that may responsibly be stated under `USO/l5/research/findings/`. Whatever is filed compares a claim to evidence, interprets, criticizes, or accepts, rejects, qualifies, or supersedes; it may request revision from a lower owner without silently mutating that owner's object. The output form is the layer's: a bounded claim/decision with cited dependencies, limitations, review status, and reproducibility information for L6 retention and L7 presentation.

## Why it exists

Keeps inquiry and interpretation separate from institutional participant structure. The research record is about what is claimed and on what evidence; who holds which participant position is filed at `USO/l5/institution/`.

## Holds

- distinct lines of inquiry — `USO/l5/research/lanes/`
- review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims — `USO/l5/research/review/`
- findings that may responsibly be stated from the available work and evidence — `USO/l5/research/findings/`
- literature search and relevance judgment, and the disclosure of methods, exclusions, uncertainty, and inferential limits that each claim carries

## Does not hold

- the work product itself: writing, experiments, computation as performed — `USO/l4/work/`
- exact relations, operators, proofs — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/`
- approximation, uncertainty models, tolerances as engineering objects — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/`
- admissible states — `USO/l1/state-space/`
- locally authored definitions — `USO/l0/semantics/definitions/`
- institutional participant positions — `USO/l5/institution/`
- retained history, superseded or retired material — `USO/l6/`
- external provenance, governance, peer bindings, and released publications — `L7/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 may interpret externally owned definitions, but interpretation does not transfer ownership of the peer definition into L0. The layer's hard boundary applies to everything filed here: consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact. A claim filed here is no broader than evidence/maturity.
