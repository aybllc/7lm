# USO/l5/research/review/

**Object:** Directory for review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims.
**Path:** `USO/l5/research/review/` · **Layer:** L5 — RESEARCH / DISCOURSE · **Owner:** USO/l5/

## What this directory is

This is the directory for review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims. Review compares a claim to its evidence, criticizes it, and accepts, rejects, qualifies, or supersedes it. Review and audit apply the layer's acceptance check: the claim is no broader than evidence/maturity, and methods, exclusions, uncertainty, and inferential limits are disclosed. The claim under review stays where it was filed — in its lane at `USO/l5/research/lanes/` or as a finding at `USO/l5/research/findings/` — and the review is filed here, naming it.

In practice a person filing here names the claim reviewed and its cited dependencies, states the review's outcome and its review status, and, where the review finds a defect in a lower-layer object, requests revision from that lower owner without silently mutating it. A review does not repair the object it reviews; the correction belongs to the owner of the layer where the invariant or contract failed.

## Why it exists

Keeps review activity distinguishable from the findings being reviewed. A reader can then see a finding, the review of it, and the review status the finding carries as three separate things.

## Holds

- review, criticism, audit, and qualification of research claims
- the review status a claim carries, as the layer's output requires ("cited dependencies, limitations, review status, and reproducibility information")
- the outcome of a review of a claim — accept, reject, qualify, or supersede (the layer's permitted actions as applied to the claim reviewed)
- requests for revision addressed to a lower-layer owner
- everything awaiting the owner's review, of any kind, until it is taken up, filed, or set aside labelled PARKED (the catch-all; see below)

## Does not hold

- the claims and lines of inquiry under review — `USO/l5/research/lanes/`
- the findings under review — `USO/l5/research/findings/`
- the L4 evidence a review re-checks — `USO/l4/work/`
- proof or measurement; review cannot supply either — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/`, `USO/l4/work/`
- peer participant positions, including a reviewer's institutional position — `USO/l5/institution/peers/`
- superseded reviews and retired claims — `USO/l6/`
- external/public legal and access governance — `L7/governance/`

## The catch-all

Owner's rule, 2026-09-06, quoted as written: "research/review/ works as a catch all". In every 7LM repository this position is also where everything awaiting the owner's review lands, however much of it there is. What the owner sets aside stays here labelled PARKED. What the owner takes up moves to `USO/l5/research/lanes/`, and back if set down again. Finished work is filed at its layer. Nothing is deleted. No other position and no new directory is needed for this; the topology already has the two moves and the exits. The tree states no rule about what an intelligence may or may not touch (owner's direction, 2026-09-06).

## Boundary / traversal rule

L5 may interpret externally owned definitions, but interpretation does not transfer ownership of the peer definition into L0. Consensus, prose, authority, or usefulness cannot substitute for proof, measurement, or a lower-layer fact.
