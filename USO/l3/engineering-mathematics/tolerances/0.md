# USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/tolerances/

**Object:** Directory for declared tolerances and admissible engineering bounds.
**Path:** `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/tolerances/` · **Layer:** L3 — ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS · **Owner:** USO/l3/

## What this directory is

"Directory for declared tolerances and admissible engineering bounds." A record here declares the bound within which an engineering representation is accepted as preserving the exact L2 relation it approximates or implements — the "declared bounds" of the layer's acceptance check, "Required L2 invariants are preserved within declared bounds." The owned item is "tolerance"; the permitted actions that land here are "bound error" and "state operating assumptions and valid region."

In practice the record names the L2 relation or invariant the tolerance is declared against, the engineering representation it applies to, the bound itself with units and precision, and the operating region in which the bound is admissible. A tolerance is a declared acceptance bound; it is not the measured or estimated uncertainty of a result, which is filed in `../uncertainty/`, and it is not evidence that the bound was met, which comes from `USO/l4/`.

## Why it exists

"Keeps acceptance bounds separate from the exact L2 relation they approximate or implement." Tolerance is not an L2 ownership matter; the L2 sheet states that "Engineering tolerance, numerical approximation, and numerical uncertainty belong to L3, not to L2 exact formal ownership." This directory is where that tolerance is owned and declared.

## Holds

- declared tolerances against named L2 relations or invariants
- admissible engineering bounds and the operating region in which they apply
- the units and precision in which each bound is stated
- the assumptions under which a bound is declared admissible

## Does not hold

- the exact relation or invariant the tolerance is declared against — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- the approximation or representation the tolerance applies to — `../approximation/`
- the error or uncertainty characterization of that representation — `../uncertainty/`
- the test, run, or measurement showing whether a bound was met — `USO/l4/work/`
- an externally owned tolerance standard relied on for a bound — bound through `L7/`, not copied here

## Boundary / traversal rule

L3 owns engineering approximation and feasibility; it does not own the realized L4 output. A declared tolerance tells `USO/l4/` "what can be built/run and within what conditions"; it does not report what was built or run.
