## What this directory is

"Directory for approximations and discretizations used to make exact relations workable." An approximation filed here stands in for an exact relation owned at `USO/l2/`; a record here makes its approximation and precision explicit, states its "operating assumptions and valid region", and shows that "Required L2 invariants are preserved within declared bounds." The layer's permitted actions that land here are "approximate, discretize, … choose representation" and "compare candidate methods to L2 invariants."

In practice the record must satisfy the layer's acceptance check: the approximation's error is explicit (its characterization may live in `../uncertainty/`), "Required L2 invariants are preserved within declared bounds", and any claim about cost is "analyzed or labeled estimates." The approximation does not replace the L2 relation and does not become exact by being used.

## Why it exists

"Makes loss or approximation visible instead of letting it appear as exact mathematics." When an exact relation is discretized or replaced by a workable method, the loss is written here where it can be inspected, rather than absorbed into a result at `USO/l4/`.

## Holds

- approximations and discretizations of exact L2 relations
- discretizations and the chosen numerical representation and precision
- the operating assumptions and valid region of each approximation
- comparisons of candidate approximation methods to L2 invariants

## Does not hold

- the exact relation itself — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- the exact operator or constructor being approximated — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/operators/`
- proof that the exact relation holds — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/proofs/`
- the uncertainty or error characterization of the approximation — `../uncertainty/`
- the declared tolerance the approximation must meet — `../tolerances/`
- the computation, experiment, or writing that realizes the approximation — `USO/l4/work/`

## Boundary / traversal rule

L3 owns engineering approximation and feasibility; it does not own the realized L4 output. "Inspection: L4 evidence may force re-evaluation without silently changing L2" — a failed approximation is re-evaluated here; the exact relation at `USO/l2/` is revised only explicitly.
