# USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/uncertainty/

**Object:** Directory for uncertainty and error characterization associated with an engineering representation.

## What this directory is

"Directory for uncertainty and error characterization associated with an engineering representation." A record here characterizes the error or uncertainty of a named engineering representation — an approximation, discretization, numerical representation, or method held under `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/` — and states how that characterization was obtained. The layer's permitted actions that land here are "estimate, bound error" and "state operating assumptions and valid region"; the owned items are "error/uncertainty budgets, sensitivity, stability, convergence, complexity" (complexity claims are held at `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/`).

In practice the record names the representation it belongs to, gives the error or uncertainty budget with its units and precision, and states the region over which the budget is claimed to hold. It does not create empirical evidence: a measured uncertainty comes from work at `USO/l4/` and returns here as "L4 evidence [that] may force re-evaluation without silently changing L2." An uncertainty is not a tolerance; the declared bound a result must meet is filed in `../tolerances/`.

## Why it exists

"Keeps uncertainty explicit rather than silently absorbing it into a result." The acceptance check for the whole layer requires that "Error/uncertainty, units, precision, and tolerance are explicit"; this directory is where the uncertainty part of that check is met.

## Holds

- error and uncertainty budgets for a named engineering representation
- sensitivity, stability, and convergence characterizations
- the method by which an error was bounded or an uncertainty estimated, with its assumptions
- the valid region over which the characterization is claimed

## Does not hold

- the approximation or discretization being characterized — `../approximation/`
- declared tolerances and admissible engineering bounds — `../tolerances/`
- the exact relation, whose exactness carries no engineering uncertainty — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/relations/`
- the measurement, run, or observation that produced an empirical figure — `USO/l4/work/`
- an externally owned uncertainty standard or guide relied on by a method — bound through `L7/`, not copied here

## Boundary / traversal rule

L3 owns engineering approximation and feasibility; it does not own the realized L4 output. "L3 is not the realized L4 output and does not itself create empirical evidence."
