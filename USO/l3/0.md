# USO/l3/

**Object:** Approximation, numerical representation, tolerance, uncertainty, and feasibility.
**Path:** `USO/l3/` · **Layer:** L3 — ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS · **Owner:** USO/l3/, the owner of engineering approximation and feasibility applied to the L2 formal contract.

Layer 3 is the "Layer 3 container for numerical and engineering feasibility applied to the L2 formal contract." It receives the exact contract from `USO/l2/` together with declared constraints, and returns to `USO/l4/` a specification of what can be built or run and within what conditions. It does not own the realized output of `USO/l4/`, and it does not create empirical evidence. Its core question is: "How can the exact L2 relation be made feasible under declared constraints?"

- algorithms and abstract data-structure choices
- units, numerical representation, precision, approximation, discretization, tolerance
- error/uncertainty budgets, sensitivity, stability, convergence, complexity
- simulation, optimization, feasibility arguments

### Permitted actions
- approximate, discretize, optimize, estimate, bound error, choose representation
- compare candidate methods to L2 invariants
- state operating assumptions and valid region

### Acceptance / boundary check
- Error/uncertainty, units, precision, and tolerance are explicit.
- Required L2 invariants are preserved within declared bounds.
- Resource/complexity claims are analyzed or labeled estimates.

## In the case of

### Working life boundary hypothesis

L2 may formalize exact relations about life, but L2 does not mathematically support life.

Mathematics that depends on continuation, tolerance, uncertainty, adaptation, feedback, or environmental exchange begins at L3. L4 realizes or records the resulting conversion.

**Clarification.** This boundary remains a research hypothesis until tested.

**Author note.** INFINITE is retained as a working term for open-ended tolerance/refinement across implementations and time, not as permission to move exact L2 objects here.

This hypothesis is carried here exactly as the specification marks it. It is not resolved by this sheet, and nothing filed under `USO/l3/` tests it by being filed.

### Analytic views that include L3

The specification's analytic-view table names two views in which this layer is examined with its neighbours. They "indicate traversal, dependency, or analytic view; they do not create child-directory containment."

- "L3 / L2 / L1 technical-possibility bundle — Engineering mathematics, exact formalization, and admissible state space can be examined together when the question requires all three dependencies."
- "L3 / L2 nested sub-bundle — Within the broader L3-L2-L1 view, L3-L2 is a tighter engineering/formal sub-bundle when L1 is already pinned and not itself under question."

**Author note.** These bundles are semantic and dependency views, not filesystem nesting and not mandatory traversal paths. The same scientific object may be railed through different subsets of the architecture according to the question being asked, while each representation remains owned by the layer that can state it correctly.

### An architecture as an object of inquiry

The specification's OSI-origin table states what this layer may ask or represent: "L3 - Engineering Mathematics — Engineering constraints, tradeoffs, approximation, tolerances, uncertainty, feasibility, or other engineering conditions that shaped or test the architecture." "The contained architecture is not remapped merely because the inquiry expands."
