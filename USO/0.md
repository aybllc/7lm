## What the USO is

The USO is the compartmentalized scientific interior that allows the components/representations of a bounded thing to occupy their correct layer without forcing the entire repository or corpus to share one maturity status. General repository infrastructure is not automatically a layer.

A bounded exemplar can be written or made at L4 while its semantic, state-space, formal, engineering, research, memory/history, provenance/governance, and surface representations remain separately addressable. The layer tree is the discourse structure of a thing, not a command to refile unrelated repository infrastructure.

## Seven-layer count and harmonization rule

L0 through L6 are the seven interior dependency layers. L7 is the numbered external Surface / Forward Face; it participates in traversal but is not an eighth interior dependency layer.

The owner's definition `USO/l0/semantics/definitions/FIRST_IS_ALWAYS_0.md` (FIRST is 0) is why those two sentences agree: the seven layers are the seven semantics FIRST through SEVENTH and the seven magnitudes `l0` through `l6`, so the seventh layer is `l6`; 7 is the magnitude of the desk's position, the eighth reached in traversal.

The coherent principal L0-L7 information sheets control. Repeated contracts and contradictory anchors are superseded; unique notes, cases, rationales, and boundary research are preserved. Questionable inherited concepts are reserved with topology unadjudicated unless this specification expressly places them.

## Topology

L7 is outside the USO and is physically a sibling boundary to USO/. L6 through L0 are the scientific interior. Physical siblinghood and semantic sequence are different relations: crossing L7 <-> L6 is a boundary/peering event, not filesystem nesting. In this repository `L7/` and `USO/` are siblings under the repository root; `L7/0.md` describes the forward face.

The canonical research tree keeps the interior physically flat: l6/, l5/, l4/, l3/, l2/, l1/, and l0/ are siblings under USO/. The arrows, groupings, and visual bands in this outline indicate traversal, dependency, or analytic view; they do not create child-directory containment.

## Two valid directions

Authorized external inspection inward:

L7→L6→L5→L4→L3→L2→L1→L0

Scientific construction outward:

L0→L1→L2→L3→L4→L5→L6→L7

Direction rule. Scientific construction proceeds from L0 outward only as dependencies are established. External inspection begins at L7 and proceeds inward. Description does not become prescription merely because the structure can be traversed.

## Architecture-wide dependency rules

Higher layers do not silently repair lower layers. If a higher-layer object requires different lower-layer meaning, the lower-layer dependency is revised explicitly rather than reinterpreted in place.

Layer ownership is local. A topic may appear at several layers, but each representation belongs at the lowest layer where that representation can be stated correctly.

Change one layer at a time. A correction at one layer does not silently rewrite another layer.

A canonical branch may remain empty when unused. Presence in the agnostic topology establishes admissible capability, not mandatory population.

Directory metadata may enforce a machine rule, but it does not create the human semantic meaning carried by /0. Conversely, /0 does not substitute for machine instructions.

Operational containment and analytic expansion are different relations. A complete architecture may remain owned at one 7LM layer while questions about its origin, justification, development, or effects generate representations at other 7LM layers. The contained architecture is not remapped merely because the inquiry expands.

## Self-description and machine metadata

Standing. Each local 0.md first identifies the directory/object and its path, then supplies that directory's semantic description. Machine instructions belong to the directory metadata, not to the semantic prose.

/0 carries human-readable meaning; metadata carries machine-actionable instruction. Neither replaces the other. This separation keeps semantic description distinct from machine operation.

Not instantiated in this repository: current research practice remains manual; no directory metadata, automation, or validation is enabled here. The one CI check, `.github/workflows/integrity.yml`, is repository infrastructure and not a layer (no unresolved conflict markers; the root canonical, the same in every 7LM repository; every sheet headed by its path); it moves nothing. Machine-readable metadata may support automation, but the architecture does not assume that automation has been enabled or validated. Current research practice may remain manual until the metadata contract is tested.

## Direction

Construction enters at `USO/l0/` and proceeds outward through `USO/l1/` … `USO/l6/` toward `L7/`; inspection enters from `L7/` and proceeds inward through `USO/l6/` toward `USO/l0/`. Scientific construction/prescription proceeds outward: L0 → L1 → L2 → L3 → L4 → L5 → L6 → L7, but only as far as established dependencies justify. Interpretive inspection of an externally owned object proceeds inward: L7 → L6 → L5 → L4 → L3 → L2 → L1 → L0.
