## What this directory is

"Directory for experiments as actually executed, including observations, measurements, tests, and failures." An experiment filed here is the experiment as run, not as designed. The specification's Experiment case fixes the owners: "Executed experiment, observation, measurement, and raw work product are L4; engineering method/tolerance L3; exact relations L2; possibilities L1; interpretation/conclusion L5."

In practice, a person filing here files the run performed and its raw work product — observations, measurements, tests, and recorded failures — with traceable inputs, conditions, transformations, results, and rework. This is the energy-to-IP direction of the conversion rule: "an energetic event, operation, measurement, observation, success, or failure is captured as an identifiable intellectual object." The governing measurement design stays at `USO/l3/`, and the result is recorded "without converting it into a conclusion".

## Why it exists

"Distinguishes performed experiment from L3 measurement design and L5 interpretation."

The same experiment has three owners: its design is engineering mathematics, its execution is conversion, its meaning is research.

## Holds

- Experiments as actually executed, including observations, measurements, tests, and failures.
- Energy-to-IP capture: observations, measurements, records, datasets, failures, and other identifiable intellectual objects.
- Raw work product of an executed experiment, with traceable inputs, conditions, transformations, results, and evidence.
- Conformance verification and rework against the governing input contract.

## Does not hold

- Measurement design, engineering method, tolerance, uncertainty, and approximation — `USO/l3/engineering-mathematics/`.
- Exact relations — `USO/l2/formal-mathematics/relations/`.
- Possibilities and admissible states — `USO/l1/state-space/`.
- Interpretation or conclusion — `USO/l5/research/`.
- External source or lineage of collected data — `L7/provenance/`; internal development history — `USO/l6/history/`. (Data case: "Collected/stored instances and transformations are L4; engineering representation/precision L3; formal encoding may be L2; state categories L1; external source/lineage is L7 provenance; internal development history is L6; interpretation is L5.")
- Writing and computation as run — `USO/l4/work/writing/` and `USO/l4/work/computation/`.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Writing belongs to L4 as performed work. Its research meaning belongs to L5; a released publication belongs to L7. Contained architectures may expand internally without changing their 7LM owner. When the question is how the OSI architecture operates, the complete OSI L1-L7 stack remains inside 7LM L4, and OSI L7 is not 7LM L7. When the question instead concerns OSI origins, development, justification, standardization, or design constraints, the inquiry may expand across the appropriate 7LM layers without remapping the OSI stack itself. An OSI network fault is therefore an L4 operational fault unless separately shown to affect another layer's owned object.

Acceptance at this directory is the layer's: "Governing lower dependencies are pinned and resolvable. A distinguishable IP/energy conversion output or recorded failure exists. Inputs, conditions, transformations, results, and evidence remain traceable. L4 existence does not authorize its own L5 interpretation."
