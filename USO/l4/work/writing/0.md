# USO/l4/work/writing/

**Object:** Directory for writing and document production as performed work.
**Path:** `USO/l4/work/writing/` · **Layer:** L4 — LAYER 4 · **Owner:** USO/l4/

## What this directory is

"Directory for writing and document production as performed work." A document filed here is filed as a thing made: the act and the produced file. The specification's Writing case fixes the four owners of one written thing: "The act/file production is L4. The research argument is L5. Internal version and correction history are L6. Public provenance, governance, and the released publication are L7."

In practice, a person filing here files the produced document and what makes its production traceable — inputs, conditions, transformations, and rework — under pinned and resolvable lower dependencies. The filing does not state what the document establishes; that is an L5 matter. "Writing is L4 as work. An exemplar/model/thesis/file is made here; its research meaning may be L5 and its released publication may be L7."

## Why it exists

"Distinguishes making the document from its L5 research meaning and its L7 released publication."

Filing a document here asserts that it was produced; it asserts nothing about its research standing or its release.

## Holds

- Writing and document production as performed work: manuscripts, exemplars, models, theses, and files as made, with their production record.
- Recorded failures of writing work, filed as identifiable intellectual objects.
- **The harmonized specification documents.** The Harmonized Authoritative Architecture Specification of the Seven-Layer Model, version of 7 September 2026, is filed in this directory as produced writing, in two files:
  - `USO/l4/work/writing/7lm-harmonized-architecture-specification.md` — the canonical Markdown form and the source of the version;
  - `USO/l4/work/writing/7lm-harmonized-architecture-specification.docx` — generated from that Markdown with built-in Word styles only (Title, Headings 1–3, Normal, Quote, Caption, List Number, Table Grid, and a monospace Code style), every top-level section starting a new page, and reading back to the Markdown unchanged. It is the draft of the next controlling document, awaiting the owner's hand; once the owner edits it, the document controls the wording again and the Markdown is re-normalized from it.
  The specification states the model: what the layers are and where each one stops, the count rule, the two directions, the canonical tree, the rules that hold across layers, and how a definition owned elsewhere is carried in. It does not restate the sheets. Each layer's full contract, and the prose and rationale of each directory, stand at that directory's own `0.md`; where a sheet and the specification differ, the specification controls. It does not carry its own history and does not carry the current condition of any repository; its versions, the dated lists of what changed, the owner's words behind each rule, and the inherited terms with their dispositions are at `USO/l6/history/specification-history.md`, and prior versions are retired under `USO/l6/retired/`.
  This placement follows the specification's own Writing case: the act/file production is L4. The specification's research meaning is an L5 matter, and its release state is an L7 matter; filing here asserts neither. The owner may re-place these files. The specification's Authority box states that it "states the controlling 7LM research architecture"; filing here does not alter that statement.

## Does not hold

- The research argument, interpretation, review, or teaching content of a document — `USO/l5/`.
- Internal version and correction history of a document — `USO/l6/history/`; superseded drafts and prior versions — `USO/l6/retired/`.
- The released publication object — `L7/publications/`; public provenance — `L7/provenance/`; governance — `L7/governance/`.
- External definitions quoted in a document: "Owner remains external at L7 peering; source/version/authority/prior-art relation is L7 provenance; applicable legal/access conditions are L7 governance."
- Experiments and computation as run — `USO/l4/work/experiments/` and `USO/l4/work/computation/`.

## Boundary / traversal rule

Writing belongs to L4 as performed work. Its research meaning belongs to L5; a released publication belongs to L7. Contained architectures may expand internally without changing their 7LM owner. When the question is how the OSI architecture operates, the complete OSI L1-L7 stack remains inside 7LM L4, and OSI L7 is not 7LM L7. When the question instead concerns OSI origins, development, justification, standardization, or design constraints, the inquiry may expand across the appropriate 7LM layers without remapping the OSI stack itself. An OSI network fault is therefore an L4 operational fault unless separately shown to affect another layer's owned object.

The Publication case states the same partition for a document that is released: "Production is L4; scholarly/research meaning is L5; internal history is L6; provenance, public/legal governance, and the released publication object are L7."
